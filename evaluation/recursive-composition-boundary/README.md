# Recursive Composition Boundary Validation

This bounded instrument falsifies the owner boundaries selected by the
recursive-composition research package. It covers:

- complete, acyclic recursion-facing instance coverage with exact occurrence
  authority and no inferred hash assumption;
- a closed, setup-fixed incremental-composition family with public selection,
  a description digest derived over the complete body, exactly grounded advice,
  and no caller-supplied digest or runtime structure generation;
- exact two-run/two-instance `EquationGrounding` with three independently
  checked equality legs;
- an exact two-slot, owner-local Relations-result source path into Analysis,
  including forged-result, reconstructed-binding, cross-coordinate, missing-
  slot, and wrong-family refusals, plus a separate one-slot CycleFold path;
- a positive two-source-to-one-target preparation executed by the existing
  Plan owner model;
- serialized value re-admission through a decider without recreation of a
  same-process causal capability;
- exact theorem-derived obligation retention and the distinct conditional,
  carried-obligations-discharged, and hypothesis-free report states;
- the CycleFold strictness, public-I/O, and same-step-fold guardrail; and
- an exact theorem application through Analysis semantic basis, support,
  validation, and judgment records whose model, two depth axes, assumptions,
  conclusions, source validation, and recurrence discipline cannot be replaced
  by a finite live chain.

Run from the repository root:

```sh
python3 -B evaluation/recursive-composition-boundary/run.py --check
```

The positive and mutation cases are finite executable models of selected
distinctions. They are not implementations of the durable owner operations,
protocol-family conformance, relation satisfaction, folding correctness,
collision resistance, random-oracle security, an induction theorem, IVC/PCD
security, or deployment support. The primary-source hashes are intake
provenance; they do not certify the papers or prove that the model is a
complete interpretation of them.

The evaluator intentionally compresses the durable carrier. Python strings
stand in for bounded semantic key and type values; carried-obligation bindings
are nested directly in the theorem fixture rather than compiled from a separate
native-rule payload; candidate-only mutation flags participate in its local
digest; and `CompositionDecisionContract` is a nested finite record, not a new
semantic-subject kind. These choices make the named falsifiers executable but
are not publication formats or identity precedents. Private issuance
registries stand in for owner-held process generations and result capabilities;
the durable design still requires the complete Foundation owner-local envelope,
owner result capability, purpose-bound source capability, and exact Analysis
manifest/support formation.
