# Authoring proofs from reusable components

Start with the maintained [source-construction guide](source-construction.md).
Its model clients demonstrate the current author boundary over the shared
Region/Process semantics, quantitative operation laws and named helper reuse.

A mathematical interface fixes its interpretation and promises. A companion
supplies mathematical choices, representation relations, admissibility premises
and proofs. Common infrastructure must associate those laws with actual source
operations and compose structural execution. Hashes and lookup names locate
candidates; checked terms establish applicability.

Pure value equations require a proved pure and total computation on their stated
domain. Effectful components require outcome-aware contracts, including service
state, order, stops and sampling. A representation relation need not be a unique
decoder, and existence of a mathematical witness is not an executable extractor.

Current construction handles supported source structure with the shared rules.
The actual-source integration and complete generated-controller connection remain
open. Authors should not reintroduce source visitors, selected slot lists, cursor
proofs or generated-controller bookkeeping while those boundaries are developed.
The former body/presentation generator recipes are retired.

See [components](../spec/components.md), [verification](../spec/verification.md)
and [support](../support.md) for the distinction between a reusable law, coverage
of a declaration promise and a proof of a fixed security request.
