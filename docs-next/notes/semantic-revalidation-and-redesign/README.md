The review found plausible gaps in Fiat--Shamir statement and transcript
closure, adversarial execution, foundational identity terms, Relations,

## Work sequence

```text
R0  preserve and reconstruct the live baseline
R1  adjudicate every finding and restate core invariants
R2  build real protocol witnesses and precise negative mutations
R3  perform question-driven primary research and candidate redesign
R4  select one integrated semantic model
R5  normalize, split, deduplicate, and absorb documentation
R6  re-synthesize the complete architecture and obtain independent validation
```

- **R0:** Record the exact tree, prior decisions, non-claims, open questions,
  review sources, and current implementation correspondence. Keep present-code
  defects separate from ideal-target design.
- **R1:** Classify each finding as `Confirmed`, `PartiallyConfirmed`,
  `Rebutted`, `Open`, or `SupersededByBroaderFinding`; retain evidence, scope,
  affected decisions, falsifier, repair classes, and durable owner.
- **R2:** Start with the repository's FRI grinding fixture, then apply the
  selected differentiated portfolio across Sigma/Schnorr, Sumcheck/GKR,
  native FRI/IOR, polynomial commitments, pairing-based and IPA arguments,
  folding/recursion, recent variants, and withheld frontier cases. Pair deep
  positive inhabitants with mutations that must fail at named semantic
  boundaries; use constructive or boundary analysis where execution would add
  cost without resolving a shared decision.
- **R3:** Derive bounded research questions from those failures, consult primary
  theory and comparable systems, compare equal-resolution alternatives, and
  retain the current model as a strong candidate rather than a constraint.
- **R4:** Select coherently across Foundation, PIR, Fiat--Shamir/composition,
  Relations, Analysis, and Compiler; record rejections, reversal conditions,
  exact non-claims, and inactive downstream pressure.
- **R5:** Define truly shared identity, regime, value, ABI, admission, result,
  capability, persistence, replay, and resource mechanisms once without making
  Foundation a universal semantic owner. Keep domain predicates with their
  owners. Absorb conclusions, not research narratives.
- **R6:** Re-derive the path from relation/statement/context through Core,
  Fresh/FS Protocols, Interface/Plan, correspondence/grounding, Analysis,
  Compiler, and the dormant OIR/Realization boundary. Audit identity, authority,
  information loss, failures, bounds, replay, consumers, and cycles.

## Working rules

1. The cycle is explicitly active; keep Stage 4B inactive until a separate
   decision authorizes it.
2. Treat reviewer agreement, document length, and scenario count as inputs, not
   semantic results.
3. Do not preserve compatibility at the expense of the ideal model.
4. Do not rewrite a durable specification before its invariant, witness,
   research, and candidate comparison are ready.
5. Resolve a finding only after its owner, law, executable pressure,
   cross-document effects, and negative boundary are accounted for.
6. Reduce duplication through definition ownership and semantic boundaries,
   not arbitrary line quotas or lost precision.
7. Keep semantic gaps, implementation defects, research unknowns,
   documentation defects, and process failures distinct.
8. No durable page may depend semantically on this package.

## Independent validation

Request external falsification after review adjudication, after semantic freeze,
and after documentation normalization. Provide frozen inputs, exact claims and
non-claims, witnesses, known gaps, and targeted rebuttal questions. The final
cold-reader review receives only durable documents and must reconstruct the
model without temporary notes.

## Known risks and non-claims

**Caution:** Bound every research question by scope, candidate portfolio,
witnesses, reversal conditions, and an exit decision. Premature cleanup may
hide unresolved semantics; delayed cleanup may create duplicate authority.
Independent models may share mistakes, so findings require repository evidence,
primary sources, explicit adjudication, and executable falsification.

This charter establishes no correctness, completeness, security, theorem
applicability, protocol support, implementation correspondence, migration,
schedule, or final-architecture claim.
