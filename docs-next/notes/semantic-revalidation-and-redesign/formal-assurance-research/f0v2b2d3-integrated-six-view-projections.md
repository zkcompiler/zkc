## 1. Question and answer

The package asks one question: do a typed owner path and an independent cold
canonical-byte path agree on all six normalized owner-view bodies for each of
the five D1 integrated carriers, and how many of F2-O1's
`operational-owner-view` gaps close when its checker is rerun against the
complete leaf universe?

The answer is affirmative at the bounded research boundary. All 30 body pairs
are byte-equal and round-trip. Their exact SHA-256 digests, byte lengths, and
leaf counts are frozen. The complete universe has 8,402 active leaves.

Against that universe, all 40 F2-O1 `operational-owner-view` gaps close. The
other classes do not move: 16 operational semantic/provider gaps and five
property premises remain `CannotAnswer`.

## 2. Existing-owner composition

D3 adds no top-level owner. It composes the constructor-local laws already
selected and tested by B1--B5B2:

```text
exact candidate profile
  -> D1 Core and Fresh Protocol admission
  -> B1/B2/B3/B4/B5B2 constructor-local projection laws
  -> six normalized owner-view bodies
  -> structural F2-O1 source-coordinate rerun
```

The typed path retains D1's admitted authority and unchanged PublicCoin graph
projection. B1 supplies foundation scope, binding, message, value, decision,
and runtime forms. B2 supplies all Oracle origins, modes, effects,
visibilities, and lifecycle records. B3 supplies Challenge correlation,
reduction-use, Claim, Reduction, and publication forms. B4 supplies the
admitted module-effect atom and decision-move form. B5B2 supplies Check and
expanded first-active Terminal forms, exact candidate-profile laws, and the
final six-view schema.

The independent path imports neither the typed D1 model nor the D3 typed
projector. It authenticates and parses exact Core, Protocol, semantic-module,
portable-algorithm, and evaluation-contract bytes through D1's cold route and
invokes the corresponding cold constructor encoders. Its PublicCoin body is
the unchanged D1 cold projection.

  declarations;

The baseline remains PublicCoin-eligible. The four directed D1 neighbors
remain ineligible for their original private-output, invalid module-control,
nonstatic Challenge-condition, and LogicalAccess terminal-preemption reasons.
One schema-valid owner substitution per view changes the encoded body without
changing structural schema validity, so schema acceptance is not treated as
owner truth.

## 5. F2-O1 structural rerun

D3 imports F2-O1's generator and checker files unchanged and first reproduces
their committed generated Lean and ledger. It then replaces only the 40
`operational-owner-view` gaps with exact coordinates and reruns the unchanged
checker against the cold-projected full universe.

| F2-O1 gap class | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| `operational-owner-view` | 40 | 0 |
| `operational-distribution` | 3 | 3 |
| `operational-denotation` | 6 | 6 |
| `module-effect-denotation` | 3 | 3 |
| `oracle-carrier-representation` | 3 | 3 |
| `operational-outcome-map` | 1 | 1 |
| `property-premise` | 5 | 5 |

The source count rises from 32 to 72, while gaps fall from 61 to 21. This is a
structural coordinate result. In particular, a module declaration coordinate
does not establish its provider denotation, an Oracle mode coordinate does not
select the provider carrier representation, and a Challenge domain/law
reference does not define its runtime distribution.

F2-O1's checker expects one `PublicCoinView` path namespace. The tracked D3
ledger therefore makes its compatibility adapter explicit. Virtual paths add
carrier and owner-view prefixes; each coordinate also records the actual
`carrier`, `owner_view`, and `owner_path`. The preserved compatibility
discriminator is not a relabeling of the other five owner views.

## 6. Owner-page consequence

No target text was found wrong or underdetermined by this experiment. The
normalized body definitions in the body-audit note are sufficient to compute
the bounded D1 matrix when composed with the previously checked constructor
laws. Therefore this lane proposes no owner-page delta and edits none of the
PIR, Foundation, Relations, or Analysis owner pages.

Main may use D3 as migration evidence that the six bodies compose on the D1
carriers. It should not promote the temporary projector wholesale. A durable
owner implementation still needs its own authority, lifecycle, and
correspondence checks.

## 7. Nonclaims

The affirmative aggregate is finite executable and mutation evidence. It is
not target-profile publication, arbitrary-Core projection correctness,
generated-provider correspondence, runtime execution or replay,
semantic-module or Oracle denotation, Claim or Reduction truth, theorem
applicability, protocol soundness, Fiat--Shamir security, ROM/QROM evidence,
live compiler/backend correspondence, or production readiness.

The remaining 16 operational gaps and five property premises are deliberate
`CannotAnswer` findings. Complete structural views do not turn missing
semantic or property evidence into affirmative claims.
