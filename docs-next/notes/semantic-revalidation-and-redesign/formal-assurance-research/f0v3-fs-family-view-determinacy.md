## 1. Question and answer

F0-V3 asks whether the exact current canonical-framed and duplex-sponge
sources determine every displayed field of their eight family views as an
exact finite body, while one normalized candidate grammar compiles and agrees
on every admitted executable carrier that currently exists.

The grammar half is affirmative at bounded research resolution. Eight
candidate bodies compile through recursive and iterative/topological schema
compilers in the F0-V2A finite description universe. Their expanded schemas
are identical. Typed and cold derivations agree for two checked K2 carriers and
one admitted duplex construction. Schema, law, canonical-body compiler, and
owner substitutions fail closed.

The source-determinacy half is `CannotAnswer`. Across 97 displayed fields, 37
have an exact owner-body basis, 40 are prose placeholders, and 20 use symbols
with no exact body. Neither profile manifest publishes a
`pir.static-view-schema` subject. The candidate therefore remains a proposed
description shape, not owner truth.

## 3. Normalized candidate

The candidate uses only `Atom`, `Record`, closed `Variant`, and bounded
`Sequence`. It gives explicit structure to schedule coordinates, scope
bindings, ordered influence entries, challenge ABIs and coordinates, identity
maps, duplex state and instance declarations, construction material, codec and
decoder maps, encoded coverage, execution domains, resource projections, and
schedule correspondence.

The checked-result normalization makes one important ownership correction.
The displayed `result_ref` is an owner-local process token and has no canonical

result records omit those two values and begin with:

```text

```

They then carry typed structural maps and a closed conclusion. Issuance may
continue to use an owner-local result coordinate and capability, but that
coordinate is not serialized into the view body. This is a candidate repair
for owner decision, not a claim that the existing display already says so.

## 4. Executable correspondence

The typed K2 path admits the Schnorr and Oracle fixtures, obtains affirmative
checked FS constructions, and derives all four canonical candidate values from
the typed Core, construction, and result carriers. The cold path imports no K2
model and separately derives the same values from an inert normalized carrier
record. The eight paired bodies agree exactly.

The typed duplex path admits the public construction JSON through the duplex
model. The cold path reconstructs Core, construction, and Protocol identities
and derives the three construction-owned values directly from JSON. The three
paired bodies agree exactly. The duplex witness exposes no
`CheckedDuplexFSConstruction` issuer, so the candidate duplex result schema is
compiled and mutation-tested but has no claimed witness value.

These comparisons show that the candidate is executable over the selected
finite carriers. They do not make fixture-local schedules, codec choices, or
identity maps normative PIR definitions.

## 6. Proposed owner routing

The smallest repair reuses existing PIR ownership:

```text
canonical-framed or duplex profile
  -> exact family-local view-schema subject
  -> construction or checked-result owner derives a qualified view
  -> Analysis reads that view under its own theorem-selection policy
  -> Compiler and deployment policy consume only qualified judgments
```

No new `FSKernel`, transcript-root authority, semantic MLIR token, or witness-
local owner is needed. The two families should publish separate schemas because
their state machines, material, codecs, retry/failure behavior, and checked
result records differ. Shared identity-map or coordinate subgrammars may reuse
the already selected F0-V2A algebra without importing an Interaction body by
label.

Publication should happen in the integrated F0-V2C migration after owner
selection. It must regenerate each changed family profile and its measured
dependent identity cone. This package does not compute that cone and therefore
makes no rotation-count claim.

## 7. Mutation boundary

Both schema implementations reject duplicated structural ordinals and wrong
owner subject kinds. Both value validators reject exact-law substitution and
canonical-body compiler substitution. The audit also refuses two tempting
repairs: filling a target gap from K2 or duplex witness-local values, and
copying an Interaction body solely because its field label resembles the FS
field.

The frozen mutations are discriminator evidence for the candidate grammar,
not a proof that every possible invalid grammar or value is rejected.

## 8. Nonclaims and migration consequence

This package does not publish either candidate schema, repair a target page,
establish exact current view determinacy, implement a durable owner evaluator,
prove compiler/backend correspondence, authenticate or apply a theorem, or
establish soundness, knowledge soundness, zero knowledge, ROM/QROM security,
concrete-hash security, or production support.

F0-V2C should not proceed for these family views until the eleven owner
obligations are selected or explicitly rejected. If selected, the migration
should carry the view bodies, manifest subjects, authority-envelope decision,
old-profile refusal controls, and dependent identity regeneration together.
The temporary executable package should remain evidence and should not be
merged wholesale into normative semantics.
