# F0 Current and Target Baseline

> **Kind:** Temporary reconstruction dossier
> **State:** First current/target code-spec reconstruction complete; retained as
> the baseline beneath the owner map, candidate matrix, and scenario dossier
> **Authority:** None. `current` observations defer to `docs/`; `target`
> observations defer to the cited `docs-next/` owners.

## 1. Initial observed chain

The current checkout exposes this assurance shape:

```text
Open PIR
  -> fail-closed structural sealing
  -> immutable Sealed PIR and exact local views
  -> theorem-indexed conditional soundness derivation
  -> bounded endpoint projection/emission evidence
```

**Current:** Formalization receipts authenticate an external repository,
revision, declaration, printed statement, axiom profile, and claimed covered
obligation slots. Receipt checks do not connect a concrete PIR subject to the
formal declaration, discharge a rule premise, establish theorem applicability,
or prove endpoint correspondence.

**Current:** A reduction occurrence is a structural claim-flow transformer
with an exact contract, event body, challenge requirements, checks, outputs,
and obligations. The reduction contract carries no theorem or security price;
soundness bindings select separate rule declarations.

**Current:** Interleaved bodies and shared challenges exceed the simple
off-the-shelf sequential-composition reading. The current soundness path must
use an exact composite theorem, establish a valid decomposition, or refuse to
price the group.

**Target:** The selected architecture separates `InteractiveCore`, challenge
interpretation, Protocol, Interface, Plan, Relations, Analysis, Compiler, OIR,
and Realization ownership. It deliberately permits several formal or
executable interpretations of one closed subject without granting those
interpretations production authority.

**Target:** K2 structural reduction does not establish a proof-theoretic
reduction. Relations owns relation definitions, occurrences, transforms,
refinements, satisfaction, and correspondence; Analysis owns probabilistic and
cryptographic games, reductions, assumptions, theorem bases, quantitative
loss, and property transport.

**Target:** Analysis already distinguishes semantic basis, support
instantiation, validation basis, qualified judgment, trust closure, policy,
and consumer authority. It allows direct, internal, external, certificate, or
Evidence-derived bases without claiming equal trusted computing bases.

**Target:** Full dynamic OIR and Realization remain unactivated. Build success,
fixture parity, deterministic bytes, or producer assertion does not establish
`RealizesOir`.

## 2. First positive assessment

The selected target already has four properties favorable to an ideal formal
bridge:

1. one exact closed semantic subject can support multiple interpretations;
2. structural meaning is separated from property truth;
3. proposal/production is separated from checking and reliance; and
4. missing proof authority can remain `CannotAnswer` instead of becoming an
   assumption or false result.

These properties suggest that a proof assistant or formal framework need not
become a new top-level semantic owner.

This is not yet a preservation decision. The same separation may hide an
insufficient formal subject, duplicate too many owner views, or make complete
correspondence impractical.

## 3. First design pressures

### 3.1 No checked subject correspondence

The current receipt's `covers` declaration is authored metadata. The checkout
checks the cited theorem's identity and axiom profile, not the semantic claim
that its formal subject equals or soundly abstracts one exact zkc occurrence.
This is the first assurance gap F1 must close.

### 3.2 No selected complete formal-consumer package

The target permits a formal interpretation, but an independent formal consumer
may need a complete stable neutral package that is not coupled to the MLIR
carrier. The current architecture deliberately defers that representation
until a real formal-extraction or independent-release boundary exists. F0 is
such a potential trigger and must decide whether the trigger is now met.

### 3.3 Owner-local views may be complete but expensive

Formal reasoning may need simultaneous access to Core events, Protocol
challenge interpretation, relation occurrence maps, Plan roles, Analysis
experiment coordinates, and exact environment/resource facts. Requiring a
formal adapter to join many live capabilities could preserve authority but make
portable, reproducible theorem checking impractical. Copying those fields into
one shadow Protocol would be worse. F0 must find the smallest authenticated
package or proof of join completeness that avoids both failures.

### 3.4 Operational adequacy is untested

The selected static subjects retain detailed interaction and source maps, but
there is no machine-checked demonstration that they are sufficient to define
one faithful VCVio runner under honest and adversarial handlers. Abort,
nontermination, probabilistic loss, shared Oracle state, caching, logging,
rewinding, interleaving, and shared challenges are the first pressure points.

### 3.5 The property-to-compiler seam is intentionally abstract

Analysis can express heterogeneous properties and Compiler can consume exact
qualified judgments, but no selected generic relation transports one property
through one concrete pass. F0 must avoid inventing a universal preservation
relation while still enabling proof-producing or translation-validating
compiler work.

## 4. Initial opportunity hypotheses

These are hypotheses, not selected changes.

### H1 — formal projection without semantic duplication

An owner-derived, provider-neutral formal subject package may be able to bind
the exact used views and dependency closure without entering Protocol identity.
Its identity would name the formal proposition or reification target, while an
independent checker would establish its correspondence to the admitted source.

### H2 — VCVio as the zkc-native operational substrate

VCVio's typed oracle computations and handlers may express the zkc runner and
game transformations without forcing zkc reductions into one high-level IOR
ontology. This would make VCVio an Analysis validation substrate, not the
semantic owner of PIR.

### H3 — ArkLib as a partial theorem adapter

ArkLib may remain the lower-cost provider for exact standard IOR subjects while
unsupported interleaved, shared-challenge, or zkc-native cases use VCVio-native
proofs. The adapter must be partial and fail closed; shape resemblance is not
applicability.

### H4 — proof-producing reification as the practical optimum

An untrusted exporter that emits a formal subject and a small correspondence
certificate may offer a stronger and smaller trusted boundary than either a
trusted handwritten adapter or full verification of the current compiler.

### H5 — no new top-level owner

The existing path

```text
owner subject/view
  -> Analysis proposition and validation basis
  -> qualified judgment
  -> Compiler or later Realization policy
```

may already be the right authority architecture. If so, F0 should still define
the missing subject package, correspondence relations, and trust closure rather
than adding a `FormalKernel` or theorem-root authority.

## 5. Reconstruction outcome and remaining queue

The first F0 pass has now:

1. enumerated the source closures a formal interpreter needs from PIR,
   Protocol, Interface, Plan, Relations, Analysis, Compiler, and later
   OIR/Realization;
2. traced them to canonical identities, owner views, manifests, authority, and
   refusal boundaries;
3. inspected the formalization receipt implementation/driver and confirmed
   that `covers` remains authored correspondence rather than a checked edge;
4. confirmed that F0's independently released formal-consumer scenario is one
   of the target's documented stable-neutral-package design triggers;
5. compared VCVio, ArkLib, Lean validation, CompCert, translation validation,
   Alive2, PCC, Cogent, hax, SSProve, and EasyCrypt at their actual assurance
   boundaries;
6. instantiated Candidates P/A/S/C/R at equal resolution; and
7. executed the first minimal, interleaved/shared-challenge, Fiat--Shamir,
   Oracle, Compiler, and endpoint cases on paper.

The companion F0 dossiers now close that first-pass queue: they define the
assurance lattice/trust DAG, provisional two-layer package class, F1 mutations
and independent-checker boundary, rotation cone, and staged F1/F2 contracts.
The first F2 property proposition also selected one exact VCVio declaration;
its module build and axiom probe are recorded in
[`f0-source-ledger.md`](f0-source-ledger.md).
ArkLib-wide and discriminator-specific theorem audits remain dependent F2
work, not an open condition on this baseline reconstruction.

## 6. Current non-claims

This baseline does not establish that the selected architecture is formally
adequate, that VCVio is preferable to ArkLib, that a neutral package is needed,
that any correspondence checker exists, that the current implementation
matches the target, or that any theorem applies to a zkc artifact.
