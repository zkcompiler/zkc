# F0 Charter and Research Method

> **Kind:** Temporary active work-package charter
> **State:** First F0 research-resolution pass complete; provisional
> architecture and exact F1/F2 handoff recorded, with F0 reopenable by every
> dependent falsifier
> **Authority:** None. Candidate vocabulary in this page is not a selected
> schema, owner, operation, or public name.
> **Scope:** Ideal formal-assurance architecture through the entry contracts
> for F1 concrete-subject reification and F2 operational interpretation.

## 1. Exact question

Starting without a compatibility preference, derive the smallest coherent
architecture that can support the following chain without collapsing its
judgments:

```text
exact admitted zkc subject
  -> exact formal subject and checked correspondence
  -> exact formal operational or theorem-provider subject
  -> property-specific checked theorem result
  -> qualified Analysis judgment with explicit trust closure
  -> later property-preserving Compiler and Realization consumption
```

Determine whether the selected target already owns every necessary subject and
transition, needs additive owner-local views and validation bases, or should be
structurally redesigned before normative cutover.

## 2. Scope and non-goals

F0 includes:

- the admitted Core, Protocol, Interface, Plan, Relations, and Analysis
  subjects needed to state a formal correspondence;
- interaction, Oracle, challenge, transcript, reduction, terminal, failure,
  visibility, occurrence, environment, and resource observations;
- formal-provider and checker identity, lifecycle, trust, replay, and failure;
- theorem applicability, theorem truth, subject correspondence, property
  transport, compiler preservation, and realization as separate judgments;
- VCVio, ArkLib, direct Lean definitions, proof-producing exporters,
  translation validation, and extracted checkers as alternatives; and
- the minimum stable neutral package, if any, required by an independent
  formal consumer.

F0 does not:

- choose a proof assistant by convenience or reputation;
- claim that current receipts establish subject correspondence;
- formalize the whole compiler, runtime, or cryptographic library;
- activate OIR/Realization or change the main post-freeze dependency order;
- promote a candidate into a durable owner before convergence; or
- treat a finite proof pilot as an asymptotic or cryptographic-security claim.

## 3. Research stance

Use the repository's reconstructive, generative, evaluative, and integrative
lenses. In particular:

1. reconstruct current authority and implementation correspondence before
   redesigning it;
2. derive a clean-room architecture from the desired guarantees rather than
   adding fields to the current model by inertia;
3. retain the current target as one equal candidate, not the baseline winner;
4. consider conceptual compression and new capability, not only defect repair;
5. use primary sources for theorem and framework facts and state where each
   analogy stops;
6. use small formal or executable prototypes only to falsify or discriminate
   candidates; and
7. converge across PIR, Relations, Analysis, Compiler, OIR, Realization,
   identity, and authority before proposing durable changes.

## 4. Candidate portfolio

F0 must compare at least these classes at equal resolution:

### Candidate P — preserve the selected multi-denotation boundary

Keep the admitted zkc subject unchanged. Add only provider-specific external
adapters and Analysis validation bases. This candidate wins only if every
formal observation is recoverable without a shadow Protocol and independent
checkers can bind their result to exact admitted subjects.

### Candidate A — provider-neutral formal subject views

Add exact owner-derived `Formal*View`-shaped subjects and checked reification
relations while keeping formal syntax, proofs, and theorem libraries outside
semantic identity. This is the leading alignment candidate, not a
preselection.

### Candidate S — separately identified formal semantic package

Create a stable neutral package with enough closed meaning for independent
formal consumers and long-lived producer/consumer separation. Determine
whether this is the already documented formal-extraction trigger or an
unnecessary second representation that freezes the wrong semantics.

### Candidate C — proof-carrying canonical subject

Make a checked formal correspondence or proof-carrying reification mandatory
for selected property-bearing or transformation-bearing artifacts. Evaluate
whether this enables materially stronger composition and compiler guarantees
or incorrectly couples semantic admission to one evidence regime.

### Candidate R — formal semantic center

Use a proof-assistant-native or effect-calculus subject as the canonical
semantic center and treat the current carrier as a projection. This is the
largest redesign candidate. It must be considered seriously and rejected only
for explicit semantic, authority, identity, independent-implementation, or
migration reasons.

Candidates may be split or combined when the research identifies a material
boundary, but no candidate may win through a lower-resolution description of
its costs or trust roots.

## 5. Workstreams

### W0 — authority and identity reconstruction

Map every current and target subject, owner, identity preimage, capability,
admission transition, replay rule, failure, and non-claim that a formal bridge
would read or produce.

### W1 — semantic adequacy

Test whether one exact admitted subject exposes all observations required for
honest execution, adversarial experiments, interleaving, shared challenges,
Oracle effects, relation correspondence, abort, resource bounds, and terminal
meaning. Any missing observation is classified as an absent source fact, an
owner-view gap, or a kernel defect.

### W2 — formal-provider architecture

Compare VCVio-native interpretation, ArkLib theorem reuse, direct Lean
formalization, and a provider-neutral validation basis. Separate provider
authentication, theorem truth, subject correspondence, applicability, and
consumer reliance.

### W3 — proof-producing and translation-validating boundaries

Compare a trusted exporter, an untrusted exporter plus certificate, a small
independent validator, an extracted validator, and a verified producer. State
the trusted computing base and failure semantics of each.

### W4 — property and compiler transport

Determine which proposition is preserved from formal subject to Analysis and
from predecessor to successor. Equality, directed refinement, probabilistic
distance, theorem applicability, and cryptographic property transport remain
different relations.

### W5 — implementation and migration pressure

Inspect canonical encoding, MLIR transport, current checkers, reference twins,
tests, and future OIR/Realization consumers. A migration plan cannot make code
the semantic authority, and a prototype cannot silently define the target.

## 6. Scenario portfolio

F0 uses the smallest portfolio that discriminates the architecture:

1. bounded relation-bound Schnorr as the minimal complete native subject;
2. RandomQuery or Sumcheck as a standard IOR/reduction comparison where
   ArkLib reuse may be natural;
3. one interleaved or shared-challenge composite as the zkc-native
   discriminator;
4. one Fiat--Shamir transcript/runner game as the VCVio discriminator;
5. one checked PIR transition as the translation-validation discriminator;
6. one OIR/backend boundary as a deliberately incomplete future consumer.

The cases do not define the architecture. A candidate that supports only these
fixtures must state the excluded family and latest responsible extension
point.

## 7. Falsification questions

At minimum, attempt to produce these failures:

- two distinct admitted zkc subjects map to one formal subject while a
  theorem-relevant observation differs;
- a formal exporter omits an event, occurrence, challenge dependency, Oracle
  action, failure, or terminal fact without checker refusal;
- an ArkLib theorem appears applicable by shape but fails on interleaving,
  shared challenge, relation map, or experiment quantifiers;
- a VCVio runner is trace-equivalent on honest executions but not under the
  property theorem's adversary or handler regime;
- a proof artifact or theorem name is replayed against a different subject,
  environment, policy, or checker version;
- a valid compiler rewrite preserves structure but not the selected semantic
  or probabilistic relation;
- an extracted checker changes the trusted boundary without that residual
  assumption appearing in the qualified result; and
- a provider outage, unsupported subject, malformed proof, false certificate,
  checker failure, and semantic counterexample collapse into one outcome.

## 8. F0 outputs

F0 is not complete until it produces:

1. a current-model and code/spec correspondence dossier;
2. a primary-source and framework ledger with exact versions where material;
3. a subject, owner, identity, lifecycle, and consumer map;
4. an assurance-claim lattice and trusted-computing-base map;
5. the equal-resolution candidate matrix;
6. scenario, counterexample, and opportunity results;
7. a provisional architecture with explicit changes, non-changes, residual
   trust, and reversal conditions;
8. a current-to-target gap and rotation-cone analysis; and
9. exact F1 and F2 entry contracts.

## 9. F1 entry contract

F1 may start only when F0 provisionally fixes:

- one exact admitted source subject and complete source-view closure;
- one formal-subject schema or provider-neutral proposition;
- one correspondence relation and its polarity, refusal, and trust meaning;
- identity and replay bindings for source, formal subject, checker, and
  environment;
- an untrusted-producer or trusted-producer choice with rationale;
- the bounded pilot and negative mutations; and
- the exact result that Analysis may consume, without granting authority to a
  serialized record.

F1 success establishes only its exact structural or semantic correspondence.

## 10. F2 entry contract

F2 may start after F1 has one affirmative bounded correspondence and F0/F1
have fixed:

- the operational observations and projection relation;
- randomness, Oracle, handler, adversary, termination, abort, and resource
  regimes;
- one VCVio interpretation candidate and one comparison path;
- one property theorem with exact assumptions and quantitative conclusion;
- an Analysis proposition, validation basis, trust closure, and admission
  policy; and
- a second discriminating case that prevents a sequential honest-run model
  from passing vacuously.

For this staged program, the provisional handoff pins the first property to
VCVio's concrete `Schnorr.sigma_complete` theorem, with acceptance probability
exactly `1`, at an exact revision/toolchain/manifest and reported axiom
closure. F2 still must establish provider correspondence and applicability;
the theorem pin does not pre-answer those questions.

F2 success does not establish compiler preservation, general Fiat--Shamir
security, backend realization, extraction correctness, or whole-protocol
security unless those exact relations are separately formed and checked.

## 11. Gates and stopping rule

- **Discovery:** current and ideal models, sources, alternatives, and trust
  boundaries are represented at comparable resolution.
- **Local coherence:** the preferred architecture has exact subjects,
  identities, owners, transitions, failures, non-claims, and independent-
  checker meaning.
- **Cross-domain convergence:** PIR, Relations, Analysis, Compiler, and the
  dormant OIR/Realization boundary require no contradictory authority.
- **F1 readiness:** another implementer could construct the pilot and its
  negative checker cases without interpreting research prose.
- **F2 readiness:** the operational model and property experiment are exact
  enough that a failed proof has a defined semantic disposition.

F0 stops with a provisional decision, an exact deferral caused by named
missing evidence, or a justified main-design reopening. Passing current tests
or failing to find a defect is not a stopping condition.

## 12. Intended destinations and deletion

Selected owner and architecture changes are absorbed into their exact durable
pages. Rejected alternatives with durable rationale go to a decision record;
deferred implementation goes to the authoritative roadmap. Delete this page
after every retained conclusion has one durable owner and F1/F2 have consumed
or explicitly declined their handoff contracts.
