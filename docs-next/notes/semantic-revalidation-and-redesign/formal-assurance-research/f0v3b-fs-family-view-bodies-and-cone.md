## 1. Question and answer

F0-V3B asks one exact question: do explicit candidate owner fragments close all
eleven F0-V3 description obligations as four canonical-framed and four duplex
finite schemas, compile identically by both schema compilers, produce
byte-identical values on every inhabitable typed/cold witness path, and yield
one reproducible profile-rotation cone when overlaid on the common migration
candidate?

The answer is affirmative for that candidate. The two proposal packets
reconstruct the complete eight-view source. Recursive and iterative schema
compilers agree. Typed and cold projections byte-agree on eight K2 values and
three duplex values. Both publication compilers agree on the combined
16-profile cone and on the 11-profile incremental cone attributable to the FS
overlay.

This does not change the F0-V3 current-source result. The present displays
still contain 37 exact-body fields, 40 prose placeholders, and 20 undefined
symbols. Candidate selection is an owner decision, so every original
obligation remains `CannotAnswer`.
