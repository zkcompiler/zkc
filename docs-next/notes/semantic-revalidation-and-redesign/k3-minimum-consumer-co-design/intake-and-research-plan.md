## 1. Question and method

K3-A asks one bounded question:

> What is the minimum consumer co-design needed to demonstrate that K1/K2 can
> feed Relations, property Analysis, and endpoint projection without duplicate
> authority or upstream semantic backflow?

The audit followed semantic dependencies rather than directories. It compared:

1. K1/K2 formation, identity, execution, replay, and source-view declarations;
2. every frozen minimum-consumer question in the
   [Invariant Ledger](../invariant-ledger.md#8-minimum-pre-freeze-consumer-questions);
3. the candidate Relations, Interface/Plan, canonical-carrier, Analysis, and
   OIR pages;
4. the current normative specification and corresponding implementation seams;
5. Stage 3 and Stage 4A research and selection records;
6. the executable value-bridge and protocol-witness instruments; and
7. primary sources selected only where they distinguish live alternatives.

The evidence was audited separately for Relations, Analysis, the
Interface/Plan-carrier-OIR cone, and current-system correspondence. The
synthesis below records repository-verifiable conclusions rather than review
agreement.

No downstream durable schema was rewritten in K3-A. Historical Stage 3 and
Stage 4A records remain immutable evidence of their recorded snapshots.

## 2. Closed source intake

### 2.1 K1

[Executable Semantic Foundations](../../../foundation/executable-foundations.md)
owns the shared substrate:

- regime-qualified `SemanticContentId<K>` identities over exact canonical
  bodies and used-module closure;
- domain-indexed `ValueType` and canonical values;
- small first-order portable algorithms with derived function types;
- typed semantic failures and deterministic evaluation controls; and
- separate authentication, evaluator support, admission, and checked bridge
  authority.

K3 consumers must extract their values, algorithms, identities, and bounds
from this substrate. They may define domain predicates and subject-specific
failure meanings, but may not retain the old `H(...)`,
`CanonicalSemanticValue`, `ClosedFiniteTerm`, opaque ABI, or ambient-registry
placeholders as parallel foundations.

### 2.2 K2 Interactive Core

[Interactive Core and Causal Execution](../../../pir/interactive-core.md)
owns the exact verifier-observable Core, including:

- public and verifier-private invocation inputs;
- explicit scopes and scoped `PublicBindingDecl`s classified as `Statement`,
  `SessionContext`, or `PublicParameter`;
- typed values, guarded occurrences, messages, challenges, oracle lifecycle,
  checks, claims, reductions, and explicit terminals;
- verifier-visible history, `ProverDecisionPoint`, legal `ProverMove`, and the
  causal `StrategyStep` boundary;
- `GenerateRun`, completed and noncompleted outcomes, run records, live causal
  provenance, and replay without causal provenance; and
- `PublicBindingView`, `StrategyDecisionView`, `PublicCoinView`, `EffectView`,
  `ClaimReductionView`, and `ExecutionView`.

Witness values, advice, private randomness, search state, and implementation
state are intentionally outside `CoreId`. K3 must classify them at dependent
surfaces rather than insert concrete secrets into Core identity.

### 2.3 K2 Fiat--Shamir construction

[Fiat--Shamir Construction](../../../pir/fiat-shamir.md) owns:

- an exact transcript declaration and K1 framing law distinct from wire
  serialization;
- all scoped Statement bindings and every active prior prover-controlled
  publication in each authoritative challenge prefix;
- structural public-coin eligibility and same-Core Fresh/FS identity maps;
- exact namespace, acceptance, decoding, state transition, bounded retry, and
  typed sampling-failure behavior; and
- `TranscriptDeclarationView`, `RequiredInfluenceView`,
  `ChallengeTransitionView`, and `FSConstructionView`.

Analysis may add theorem, model, adversary, and quantitative coordinates. It
may not author a second transcript or weaken the authoritative full-prefix
law. A BCS/IOR-to-Core correspondence, when needed, precedes and remains
separate from the same-Core FS construction.

### 3.1 The Plan/OIR identity contradiction

The candidate Plan makes `ProverPlanId` commit to the whole Plan, and the
candidate plan-specialized OIR commits to that ID. The same text forbids OIR
from reading `RealizationOnly` fields. This contradicts OIR-Q2: changing a
realization-only fact must not rotate OIR.

K3-B must select the structural factorization: full Plan identity cannot
automatically rotate OIR, and OIR reads must arrive through an exact
projection-purpose view or subject. K3-D must classify the exact read set
against minimum projection semantics and select how OIR identity and the
source-relative result bind it. The live alternatives are:

1. an identified projection-relevant Plan subview;
2. distinct projection and realization subjects; or
3. proof that the selected v0 Plan has no independently variable
   realization-only fields.

The likely architecture is that OIR semantic identity binds exactly the facts
it reads, while any source-relative projection result separately binds the full
admitted source tuple as provenance and checking authority. This is a
candidate, not a K3-A selection.

### 3.2 The missing run-grounding seam

K2's `RunRecord` authenticates exact execution structure but deliberately does
not retain all invocation values or prover-private material. A caller-created
relation assignment therefore cannot establish what one verifier run consumed.

K3-B must test an execution-issued, attenuated occurrence view over the exact
invocation and run. It must identify Statement occurrence, domain, value,
claim/reduction/result coordinates, and any permitted material occurrences,
while excluding unrelated secrets. A bare content ID, replay match, or caller-
constructed tuple grants no such authority.

### 3.3 The three replay relations

K3-C must keep these disjoint:

| Relation | Meaning | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| K2 execution replay | One record agrees with the deterministic Core/Protocol transition laws | Strategy invocation, causal generation, sample distribution, or adversary-class membership |
| Cryptographic rewind or restoration | A counterfactual experiment branches, resets, or restores an adversarial interaction under a theorem model | Equality to K2 replay or permission to mint a causal capability |
| Analysis cold replay | Exact semantic dependencies and checker/derivation authority are reconstructed and the judgment is rerun | Restoration of process-local causal or confidential occurrence authority |

An Analysis result whose proposition actually depends on a live causal or
confidential occurrence uses the local-authority lane and has no exact public
cold replay of that fact.

### 3.4 Narrow K2 pressure retained

The provisional non-reopen verdict does not erase three concrete questions:

1. K2 issues live causal provenance for a terminal `CompletedRun`, but not for
   the `InterpretationFailed` record variant. An occurrence-sensitive Analysis
   of FS failure mass may need a broader causal-record view, or it may use an
   Analysis-owned abstract generation relation instead.
2. K2 does not define affirmative replay or resumption of a partial run.
   Round-by-round and state-restoration arguments may need an Analysis-owned
   prefix/branch game with checked correspondence to K2 transition facts.
3. K2 exposes a prover strategy boundary under a fixed verifier. A malicious-
   verifier or multi-prover property needs a separate Analysis game or a
   supported-role extension; it must not be mislabeled as ordinary K2
   execution.

K3-C must resolve these for its selected profiles. They reopen K2 only if the
required fact is verifier-observable Protocol meaning and cannot be exported
as a faithful owner-derived view. Their existence alone is not a Core defect.

## 4. K3 application of the frozen view law

K3 applies the view law already frozen by the
[Invariant Ledger](../invariant-ledger.md#8-minimum-pre-freeze-consumer-questions):

```text
source owner
  owns fact meaning and derives an immutable view from one exact admitted source

consumer
  declares purpose + finite read manifest + adequacy requirement
  evaluates its own proposition

checked result
  binds exact sources, question, reads, checker basis, and qualified outcome
```

Missing, extra, wrong-subject, wrong-purpose, unauthenticated, unsupported, and
negative cases remain distinguishable. A consumer may combine separately owned
source views in a checked intake, but that intake is a consumer record, not
another source-owned view or a new universal fact root.

This law preserves K1 authority mechanics without making Foundation the owner
of Relations, Analysis, or OIR predicates. It also prevents Analysis from
restating Relations satisfaction and prevents OIR from treating an embedded
source ID as proof of complete projection.

  Fresh/FS construction;

  separate contracts;

### 5.2 Replace or reconstruct

- public-label and public-port Statement correspondence;
- exhaustive private-port/prover-obligation Witness correspondence;
- generic Protocol `ObjectRef` grounding unless a concrete case proves it
  necessary;

- monolithic universal Analysis fact or soundness-rule surfaces;
- old PIR event ordinals as complete OIR provenance; and
- full-Plan commitment in OIR identity when unread realization-only facts can
  vary independently.

### 5.3 Defer

- complete property-family catalogs, QROM and malicious-verifier-ZK support,
  generic composition, and final persistence schemas beyond one closing
  profile per required seam;
- exact structural Core-composition and descent checking, including the
  historical `CoreCompositionSpec` candidate, to the composition-heavy K4
  portfolio before K5; K3-B still must leave claim, reduction, scope, and
  material coordinates adequate for that pass;
- the BCS/IOR-to-Core correspondence to the P02 native FRI/IOR case in K4;
  same-Core FS construction cannot substitute for it;
- exact final MLIR operation spelling and transport encoding after the K2 field
  map is proven complete;
- full OIR grammar, optimizer laws, abstract endpoint execution, supplier
  vocabulary, concrete realization, deployment, and invocation;
- compiler scoring, selection, and broad judgment consumption; and
- implementation migration or current-to-target conformance until the target
  contracts are selected and checked.

## 7. K3-B: dependent surfaces, Relations, and carrier

### 7.1 Required decisions

K3-B must select and make constructible:

1. a role- and visibility-qualified Interface assignment lens total over every
   externally supplied `CoreInvocation` public and verifier-private input, and
   a separate complete external-Statement-to-`BindingRef` question;
2. role-qualified transport exposure for guarded messages, Oracles, failures,
   terminals, and outcomes, distinct from FS framing;
3. a Plan/strategy surface covering reachable decision points and legal moves,
   with Witness, advice, confidential context, randomness, search state,
   mutable state, and explicitly exported derived values distinguished;
4. a structural Plan/OIR factorization in which full Plan identity does not
   automatically rotate OIR and projection reads use an exact purpose-bound
   view or subject; K3-D owns the exact field classification;
5. K1-aligned relation definitions, interfaces, instances, witness
   occurrences, identities, algorithms, and a reachable regime/profile;
6. an occurrence-explicit relation-instance-to-Statement/claim graph that
   permits multiple bindings and repeated semantic values until fixtures prove
   a tighter cardinality;
7. claim/reduction input/output relation and witness correspondence;
8. exact artifact-fact, selector, comparison, and grounding-equation algebras;
9. structural versus run-grounded correspondence over an execution-issued
   source;
10. total equivalence, injective embedding, and directional priced-lossy
    projection contracts;
11. an exact carrier disposition for `InteractiveCoreBody`, `ProtocolBody`,
    the root `TranscriptConstructionId`, the separately authenticated
    `TranscriptConstructionBody`, and exact-used modules/dependencies, with
    round-trip laws over graph-carried fields and unknown/missing refusal; and
12. a target-native bounded imported-verification extension/effect shape whose
    identity-bearing fields survive the carrier and reach OIR's typed refusal.

Relations owns relation meaning, grounding, correspondence, directional
results, and satisfaction. PIR owns Core fact meanings and Interface/Plan
subjects. Analysis owns no part of K3-B loss arithmetic beyond later consuming
authenticated occurrence and collision coordinates.

The carrier work is a parallel sublane with a K3-B convergence gate. Relation
meaning is designed over the language-independent K1/K2 algebra; no physical
MLIR choice may determine that meaning.

## 8. K3-C: minimum Analysis closure

K3-C must not finalize all of Analysis. It closes one reusable ingress and two
end-to-end profiles.

### 8.1 Minimum source families

An Analysis family selects, rather than duplicates:

```text
Protocol source
  exact admitted Protocol
  selected K2 Core/FS views
  finite read manifest and adequacy

Relations source when required by the property
  exact language/relation view
  checked Statement/claim/witness correspondence
  bridge kind and loss occurrences
  optional concrete satisfaction fact only for an occurrence premise

Experiment model
  strategy/adversary interface and quantifier order
  public-coin or oracle model
  advice, randomness, scheduling, resources, outcomes, and failures

Theorem applicability
  exact theorem statement and authority
  source/target model and occurrence maps
  hypotheses, side conditions, substitutions, and typed loss transform

Concrete occurrence source when required
  exact run or failure record
  separately optional live causal provenance and replay match
```

The initial profiles are:

1. one classical public-coin Fresh-to-FS soundness transport consuming an
   exact Fresh property judgment, its exact Relations-owned language and
   Statement/claim correspondence, the source adversary/model profile, the
   exact K2 construction views, an affirmative theorem-applicability result,
   and the typed quantitative transform; and
2. one relation-bound property whose statement, claim, witness role, and
   relation event are supplied by the K3-B seam.

The Relations source is mandatory for the initial soundness profile. The
checked FS construction establishes no source property and cannot substitute
for the language, Statement, claim, or Fresh judgment.

Wrong strategy class, quantifier order, theorem model, statement
correspondence, hypothesis, occurrence, or loss parameter must change the
qualified result. One successful concrete run never proves the universal
property.

### 8.2 Explicit limits

K2 defines a fixed verifier and a prover strategy boundary. Malicious-verifier
ZK therefore remains unsupported in the initial profile unless K3-C defines a
separate Analysis-level verifier game with checked correspondence. QROM,
multi-prover independence, generic state restoration, and concrete RNG/hash
conformance similarly remain separate explicit profiles, not Boolean flags on
the first theorem.

## 9. K3-D: minimum OIR seam

K3-D closes no final OIR grammar. It defines the complete owner-derived read
contract for:

- exact admitted Protocol, Interface, endpoint role, and projection basis;
- the K3-B Plan/OIR projection-view factorization, with the exact Plan read
  partition, OIR identity effect, and projection-result binding selected and
  tested here;
- K2 static structure, scoped bindings, guards, messages, Oracles, challenges,
  checks, claims, reductions, failures, terminals, and extension effects;
- the bounded imported-verification extension/effect coordinate, every
  identity-bearing field, its discharge family, and typed endpoint refusal;
- causal decision and nonproduction obligations where the endpoint role needs
  them;
- exact FS frames, prefixes, state transitions, draw attempts, and sampling
  failure for FS endpoints;
- Interface input, transport, codec, order, guard, and malformed-boundary
  semantics; and
- exact coverage, no-phantom provenance, protected-effect/outcome
  correspondence, and typed `Unsupported`.

Projection identity binds static declarations and exact transition laws, not
concrete run samples. Runtime frames, draw receipts, failures, and guarded
occurrence results are quantified by abstract execution or checked as source-
relative behavior; concrete run data does not silently enter OIR identity.

`LocalOirValid` remains independent of `ProjectionCorrect`. Dropped,
duplicated, reordered, wrong-channel, wrong-guard, wrong-terminal, or phantom
effects must fail source-relative correctness even when the target is locally
valid. Wire codec changes do not silently change FS framing. Sampling
exhaustion is not verifier rejection, and strategy `Stop` is not a Core
terminal.

The imported-verification control must preserve every source field through the
PIR-owned view. Mutating any identity-bearing field must change the source
identity or refuse admission, and projection must return typed `Unsupported`
rather than a partial OIR until an exact discharge family is supported.

K3-D must explicitly choose whether the minimum v0 OIR seam covers only FS
endpoints and returns `Unsupported` for Fresh, or also exposes interactive
Fresh role machines. The latter is substantial Stage 4B design and is not
selected by this intake.

## 11. K3-A completion verdict

K3-A is complete because:

1. every minimum consumer question inspected by this intake has an identified
   dependency owner;
2. the pre-K1/K2 shadow subjects identified by this intake are classified for
   reconstruction, replacement, or explicit deferral;
3. the phase order prevents Relations, Analysis, or OIR from ratifying an
   uninhabited source;
4. primary research is attached to live alternatives and concrete
   discriminators;
5. K2 has an explicit, evidence-demanding reopen rule; and
6. the next package has bounded decisions and an observable exit gate.

This historical K3-A verdict did not close K3 or freeze the semantic kernel.
Bounded K3-B subsequently resolved the listed dependent-surface questions on
2026-08-27 without reopening K2; its selection and validation records own that
conclusion. K3-C minimum Analysis closure is next.
