## 1. Purpose

K3 closes the smallest downstream dependency cone needed to test whether the
bounded K1/K2 semantic kernel can be frozen without backflow. It does not
finish every downstream domain. It establishes that the kernel's exact facts
can be consumed without a carrier escape, a second Protocol schema, an ambient
read, or a consumer-authored success fact.

K3-A reconstructed that cone from:

- the closed K1 and K2 declarations and their explicit handoffs;
- the pre-K1/K2 target Relations, Interface/Plan, carrier, Analysis, and OIR
  pages;
- the current normative specifications and implementation;
- earlier Stage 3 and Stage 4A research records;
- executable protocol and value-bridge probes; and
- targeted primary research on relation interfaces, oracle reductions,
  folding, Fiat--Shamir analysis, IR conversion, and semantic preservation.

The resulting boundary and research program are recorded in
[K3-A Intake and Research Plan](intake-and-research-plan.md).

## 2. K3-A result

The K2 Core and Fiat--Shamir identity-bearing bodies remain **provisionally
closed** at their bounded scope. No inspected consumer case currently requires
a new verifier-observable or identity-bearing `InteractiveCore` fact. The PIR
consumer-export layer remains open, and several candidate consumers still name
pre-K2 ports, objects, prover obligations, event positions, whole traces, or
authored Fiat--Shamir maps that no longer own meaning.

This is not a mechanical vocabulary migration. In particular:

- Relations cannot bind its Statement and Witness surfaces until
  Interface/Plan is reconciled to K2;
- the canonical carrier cannot claim K2 correspondence until each K2 semantic
  subject has an explicit carrier disposition and every graph-carried field
  has an exact representation and inverse read law;
- Analysis cannot quantify over adversaries using a supplied trace or treat
  K2 replay as cryptographic rewinding; and
- OIR cannot establish source-relative correctness from an old event ordinal
  or commit to realization-only Plan facts.

Therefore K3-B co-designs the K2-dependent Interface/Plan surface, minimum
carrier correspondence, and Relations algebra together. Relations-first work
would otherwise ratify mappings into uninhabited source families.

## 3. Preserved architecture

K3-A preserves these decisions unless a later discriminator falsifies them:

1. K1 owns shared identity, value, portable-algorithm, failure, and bounded-
   evaluation mechanics; consumers own their predicates and subject bodies.
2. One `InteractiveCore` owns verifier-observable interaction. Fresh and
   Fiat--Shamir remain distinct interpretations over that same Core.
3. Protocol, Interface, Plan, relation attachments, Analysis judgments, and
   OIR are separately identified subjects or results with directional
   dependencies.
4. Statement, relation witness, private advice/state/randomness, proof
   transport, claim, check, and terminal outcome remain distinct roles.
5. Relation meaning, artifact interpretation, grounding, correspondence,
   satisfaction, property judgment, local OIR validity, and source-relative
   projection correctness remain separate judgments.
6. A source owner derives an immutable purpose-specific view; a consumer owns
   its complete read manifest, adequacy requirement, and proposition. A view
   never becomes a second semantic owner.
7. Structural Fiat--Shamir construction, theorem applicability, and
   property-specific transport remain separate contracts.
8. One semantic algebra has one canonical MLIR carrier, but transport parsing,
   canonical authentication, semantic admission, and downstream projection
   remain distinct.

## 5. Reopen discipline

K2 reopens only when a concrete consumer question requires a verifier-
observable or identity-bearing Core fact that cannot be derived faithfully
from its current body and views. A new relation map, theorem family, strategy
class, loss expression, carrier spelling, or projection policy is downstream
work and does not by itself reopen K2.

A proposed reopen must name:

1. the exact protocol or theorem case;
2. the missing observable;
3. why a source-owned derived view is inadequate;
4. the identity, admission, execution, and FS consequences;
5. a positive inhabitant and a same-boundary negative; and
6. the smaller downstream alternative that was rejected.

The current generic-object question remains conditional. K3-B first attempts
committed-material grounding over K2 typed values and occurrence coordinates.
Only a concrete R1CS/AIR, oracle, or folding case that cannot be expressed that
way may reopen the Core object surface.

## 7. Scope limits and nonclaims

K3 does not establish cryptographic security, theorem truth, protocol-family
completeness, implementation conformance, a final MLIR spelling, a complete
Analysis catalog, compiler policy, full OIR semantics, realization, migration,
or cutover readiness. K3-A performed reconstruction and static audits. K3-B
selected a bounded downstream schema and exercised a finite reference
instrument; it did not establish implementation conformance or prove that K2
represents every protocol in the K4 portfolio. K3-C selected the bounded
three-seam Analysis model and exercised its constructibility and refusal
boundaries;
it did not mint the abstract family's all-`n` source capability and therefore
does not currently produce the AFK transport judgment without external proof
authority. It also did not prove the imported theorem, Fresh uniformity or
correspondence, the ROM, knowledge soundness, special soundness, or any concrete
security claim.

Current implementation behavior is regression and design evidence only. A
current exact-coverage check, transcript interpreter, relation adapter, or
soundness rule does not establish correspondence to K1/K2/K3 until an exact
checked bridge exists.
