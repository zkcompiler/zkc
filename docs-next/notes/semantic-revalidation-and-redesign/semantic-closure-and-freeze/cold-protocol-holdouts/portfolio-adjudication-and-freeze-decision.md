## 1. Decision

The source-first holdout portfolio does not demonstrate a defect in the shared
semantic grammar. The frozen seventeen-profile candidate therefore advances
without an identity rotation.

This is a bounded architectural result, not a completeness claim. It says that
the assigned source material can be separated faithfully into the model's
existing owners, owner-local profiles or modules, explicit finite limits, and
honest unanswered questions. It does not say that every named proof system is
implemented, that every theorem is encoded, or that the two-role finite target
is universal.

The portfolio produces three different kinds of result that must not be
collapsed:

1. WHIR, Circle STARKs, WARPfold, and the explicit interactive Galois-ring
   components require exact owner-local semantic content but no shared repair.
2. Full multiparty Sumcheck is deliberately outside the two-role target. Its
   commitment-anchored virtual two-role proof remains representable, but the
   coalition-secure MPC execution is not.
3. The complete Galois-ring noninteractive SNARK is unanswered from the pinned
   source because that source does not specify its noninteractive transcript
   compiler. This is a source-correspondence gap, not evidence that the frozen
   transcript model is insufficient.

## 3. Shared decisions that survived

### 3.1 Interaction remains algebra-opaque

The verifier-observable interaction can carry exact values, messages,
challenges, logical-Oracle accesses, and checks without embedding polynomial,
field, circle-domain, limb, or ring laws in the shared Core.

- Circle domains and quotient-algebra function spaces remain exact Foundation
  modules and Relations laws.
- WARPfold's native/non-native field discipline remains an exact relation over
  typed limbs and bounded reconstruction.
- Galois-ring units, ideals, zero divisors, extension embeddings, and
  exceptional sets remain exact module and relation content.

The cases therefore support the rule that algebraic structure enters through
authenticated value modules, algorithms, relation profiles, and theorem
profiles. The Core must not infer field laws from a carrier name.

### 3.2 Logical Oracles remain distinct from commitments

WHIR, Circle FRI, and the Galois-ring commitment all reinforce the same
factorization:

```text
logical Oracle interaction
    -> checked commitment/opening construction
    -> committed verifier interaction
    -> checked transcript construction, when a source specifies one
```

A Merkle root, polynomial commitment, opening proof, or authenticated query is
not silently inserted into the logical protocol. Conversely, passing an
opening check establishes no binding, extractability, proximity, or theorem
truth. Those conclusions require exact Analysis premises.

### 3.3 Honest construction remains separate from verifier semantics

WHIR's folded Oracles, Circle's published trace/quotient/folded codewords and
dimension-gap scalar, WARPfold's limb and commitment material, and the
Galois-ring matrices, codes, and Merkle paths are honest-prover computations.
They belong to Plan recipes and exact Relations grounding, not to the
verifier-observable Core unless the verifier actually receives or checks them.
Circle's batch and DEEP values at queried points are instead verifier-side pure
derivations over prior answers and public claims; materializing them as new
Oracles would invent public events.

This preserves one Core across different prover implementations and prevents
an honest algorithm from becoming a verifier-side semantic requirement merely
because one source describes it operationally.

### 3.4 Reductions and correspondence remain explicit

The cases do not require a universal transition or folding algebra.

- WHIR's evolving constrained-code claim is an exact source-local reduction.
- Circle FRI and batched Circle FRI use exact domain and claim transforms.
- WARPfold uses a bounded sequential recurrence with strict and relaxed
  relation profiles, not a universal accumulator subject.
- Ring Sumcheck, ZeroCheck, Libra, and HyperPlonk use distinct exact relation
  and reduction profiles.

The shared model supplies typed claim, relation, occurrence, and correspondence
seams. Each family supplies its own mathematical law and Analysis theorem.

### 3.5 Property truth remains outside structural admission

Every case contains claims that cannot be inferred from a valid protocol
shape:

- WHIR round-by-round soundness and its conjecture-dependent variants;
- Circle FRI soundness, knowledge soundness, and the separation between a
  separately sourced randomized construction and the optimized
  non-zero-knowledge variant;
- WARPfold commitment binding, extraction, and finite-sequence soundness;
- coalition security and abort behavior for multiparty Sumcheck; and
- randomized code distance, Merkle binding, extraction, and ring-specific
  soundness for the Galois-ring construction.

These remain source-pinned Analysis subjects with exact hypotheses and losses.
The holdouts neither activate nor prove them.

## 4. Boundaries retained deliberately

### 4.1 Two-role protocol scope

The frozen Core has one Prover, one Verifier, and one PublicEnvironment. Full
multiparty protocols require participant-indexed local state and views,
corrupted coalitions, broadcast and opening semantics, correlated randomness,
ideal-functionality correspondence, and common-abort behavior. Collapsing
those actors into one prover or verifier would erase theorem-relevant facts.

The selected decision is to retain the two-role scope rather than add a role
count parameter. A future multiparty regime would be a coordinated research
program across Core, Plan, Interface/OIR, Realization, and Analysis, with new
identity and noninterference questions. It is not an owner-local profile edit.

### 4.2 Exact finite carriers

The current target forms exact finite semantic subjects. The practical domain
sizes and algorithmic carriers used by WHIR, Circle STARKs, and the Galois-ring
construction are not thereby claimed executable or economical.

This remains an intentional boundary pending a dedicated study of
algorithmically represented large carriers and Oracles. That study must
preserve canonical identity, equality, replay, resource refusal, and checked
access without hiding semantics behind an opaque callback.

### 4.3 Source-complete transcript construction

A protocol is not made noninteractive by packaging messages as proof bytes.
The challenge derivation, framing, statement binding, retry/failure behavior,
and theorem route must come from an exact construction profile and an
appropriate source.

The Galois-ring paper's omitted transform therefore remains unanswered. A
future construction may combine its interactive protocol with an independently
specified transcript compiler, but the resulting composed subject must not be
described as a literal reconstruction of an omitted source step.

## 5. Cross-case negative controls retained

The following controls survive portfolio synthesis and must remain attached to
future implementations or profiles:

- reclassifying a proof-sent Oracle as an initial Oracle;
- treating commitment/opening verification as a proximity, binding, or
  extraction theorem;
- replacing Circle-domain algebra with an ordinary multiplicative-subgroup
  polynomial fiction;
- publishing Circle DEEP or batch words as independent Oracles instead of
  deriving queried values from source words;
- treating WARPfold limbs as a universal bijective value bridge;
- independently sampling per-field challenges that the source derives from one
  bounded integer;
- reporting hidden-opening equality from public commitment equality without
  the required hypotheses;
- collapsing physical MPC participants into one role while retaining a
  coalition-security claim;
- assigning classical Sumcheck soundness to a virtual transcript whose source
  polynomial, mask, and final evaluation have no binding anchor;
- cancelling or inverting non-units in a Galois ring;
- inferring code distance from encoder execution;
- reusing the field set-consistency law over a ring with zero divisors; and
- calling an interactive transcript noninteractive without a checked challenge
  construction.

These are semantic guards, not proof that the corresponding positive theorem
holds.

## 7. Retained research program

The holdouts sharpen, rather than erase, the post-freeze program.

### Immediate evidence package

Activate the selected finite-cover Analysis discharge for fixed-extractor
universal correctness. This is the first opportunity to produce an
unconditional finite result from the framework. Its result must remain scoped
to the exact finite subject and checker; it establishes no asymptotic or
cryptographic theorem.

### Verifier-derived Oracle package

Resolve the verifier-derived-word and query-plan seam first. Circle's batch and
DEEP values join DEEP-ALI and STIR as materially different source pressure for
one reusable law. Compare explicit pure derivation, authenticated virtual
Oracle views, and static query-plan elaboration; preserve source transcript
identity and reject any candidate that invents a publication or hides an
evaluator.

### Large-carrier and preprocessing package

Research two separate candidates:

1. algorithmically represented large carriers and streaming access; and
2. pre-invocation preprocessing and checked setup.

WHIR and Circle both reach the current Oracle ceiling; the Galois-ring
code/setup construction supplies pressure for large carriers and preprocessing.
A shared primitive is promoted only if at least two materially different
sources require the same law.

### Transcript and endpoint package

Complete the duplex construction-material Plan lane, public-setup sibling, and
endpoint/OIR sibling. Decide vendor byte compatibility separately from
semantic equivalence. Use a separately pinned source before composing a
transcript construction with the Galois-ring interactive protocol. Add exact
correspondence between WARPfold's relation-embedded hash chain and the admitted
transcript framing, plus an executable set-difference distribution and bounded
retry/failure case for Circle's out-of-domain challenge.

### Transport and deployment package

Research serializable checked handoff at the Realization boundary while
retaining same-process one-use handoff as the causal default and ordinary
readmission as the portable degraded path.

### Security-property package

Create exact source-pinned property families for state restoration,
round-by-round reasoning, WHIR, Circle FRI, WARPfold and incremental
composition, and ring-specific code/commitment/PIOP claims. Activate only the
families backed by complete theorem sources and exact semantic subjects.

### Multiparty regime study

Retain a separate charter, not an implementation promise, for participant-
indexed protocols. Its first task is to compare actor, view, corruption,
communication, randomness, abort, and ideal-functionality semantics before any
shared schema is proposed. The study also decides whether authenticated secret
sharing can inhabit the checked commitment abstraction for the virtual proof
and what premise relates the coin functionality to PublicEnvironment.

## 8. Independent falsification disposition

The bounded review covered the Circle STARKs, WARPfold, and multiparty Sumcheck
records against their pinned sources and the frozen owner boundaries. It found
no freeze blocker and independently agreed with the three primary
classifications. The Galois-ring source-gap classification was outside its
scope.

Direct reconstruction confirmed three major record defects:

1. the Circle record incorrectly materialized DEEP-derived words as published
   Oracles;
2. the virtual multiparty projection assigned classical Sumcheck soundness
   without the authenticated-sharing/commitment anchor on which the source's
   final check depends; and
3. the WARPfold record placed degradation and reinforced-equality checks in
   Protocol 1's Core even though the source places them in the recursive
   circuit relation.

All three are repaired in their owner mappings and negative-control matrices.
The review's source-detail corrections were also reconstructed and applied:
Circle real/imaginary batching and ZK source separation; the initial
multiparty reduction coin; WARPfold's strict-plus-relaxed rationale, static
continuation arm, required-but-unspecified decider, and under-specified broad
cross-system application. The source ledger now includes the Circle appendix
actually used.

These repairs remove invented events and silent theorem assumptions. They make
the evidence stricter without changing a published semantic profile. The
review also promoted verifier-derived words from a generic future resource
topic to a dedicated immediate research package because Circle, DEEP-ALI, and
STIR now provide repeated source pressure.
