# Selected Recursive-Composition Boundary Model

> **Kind:** Temporary selected-design record
> **State:** Selected, falsified, and absorbed
> **Authority:** None

## Decision

zkc will not introduce a universal accumulator, recursion engine, recursive
proof object, or second execution semantics. Recursive composition is instead
an owner-separated conjunction of existing one-step semantics and four narrow
additions:

1. Relations checks total recursion-facing instance coverage and exact
   occurrence linkage.
2. Analysis authenticates one closed incremental-composition family and checks
   its uniformity contract.
3. Analysis applies an incremental-composition theorem to that exact family,
   recurrence discipline, model, independent depth axes, decider semantics,
   and retained assumptions through the existing semantic-basis, support,
   validation, and judgment path.
4. Analysis derives an exact outstanding-obligation set from the theorem and
   family; callers never author the report's obligation input.

PIR remains the owner of one generated interaction and one accepted-terminal
causal handoff. A sequence of runs does not become a new PIR object. Relations
remains the owner of value equations and exact run/instance joins. Analysis
alone interprets those structural facts under a theorem. Realization may move
portable material, but transport never recreates same-process causal
authority.

```text
one admitted PIR member + one ProverPlan
  -> one generated accepted step
  -> one exact Relations recurrence edge

closed family + recurrence discipline + theorem source
  -> Analysis semantic basis + support + validation
  -> one conditional judgment per exact property conclusion
     carrying every undischarged assumption and decider obligation
```

The upper path is executable and occurrence-local. The lower path is semantic
and theorem-qualified. Neither path derives the other.

## Six boundary decisions

### Total instance-binding coverage

**Selected owner:** Relations, with hash interpretation imported by Analysis.

A recursion-facing binding schema names a finite set of instance roles. Every
role must be discharged in exactly one of two ways:

- an exact strict check of that role's current occurrence; or
- one exact, acyclic digest-binding path ending at a strictly checked role.

Unknown endpoints, duplicate parent edges, cycles, surplus edges, and an
unbound role refuse or return a substantive negative coverage result. Equal
bytes from a different instance occurrence do not satisfy an occurrence
binding. The checked result retains the exact schema, invocation, paths, and
source authorities.

Relations establishes only path totality and the written digest equations. It
does not establish collision resistance, random-oracle behavior, or that a
digest is a binding commitment. Any theorem that uses those meanings carries
an exact Analysis assumption for each digest rule.

### Closed continuation-family uniformity

**Selected owner:** Analysis for the authenticated family and
uniformity proposition; PIR and Relations retain their referenced member
subjects and runtime checks; Compiler may select but not create members.

The finite family contains every admissible continuation member as exact
PIR Protocol/Plan and Relations subject IDs, an exact public selector table,
typed update-verifier and final-decider contracts, and any exactly grounded
family-description advice coordinate.
Formation enforces:

1. **Structure closure.** All member structures are already authenticated and
   independent of runtime instance, witness, challenge, and transcript values.
2. **Self-reference discipline.** Analysis derives a family-description digest
   over the complete family body under the declared algorithm and evaluation.
   A member may expose an ordinary advice coordinate grounded by Relations,
   but no caller supplies the digest and no member embeds its family ID or the
   derived digest output in the family body.
3. **Selection, not generation.** A public runtime selector chooses an existing
   member. It cannot supply a Core, Plan, relation body, law body, or semantic
   profile.

This family is not an execution root and owns no accumulator. Each selected
run is still generated by the ordinary PIR member named in the family.

### Theorem application

**Selected owner:** Analysis.

An ordinary Analysis theorem schema and its semantic basis bind all of:

- the exact authenticated incremental-composition family;
- the exact step/compliance relation;
- the recurrence equation and total binding-coverage discipline;
- the theorem schema and its separate source validation;
- path or finite-in-degree DAG topology and exact predecessor bound;
- the execution-depth domain and the separate compliance-predicate-depth
  domain;
- the standard, random-oracle, or other exact experiment model;
- the exact same-process, arbitrary-eligible-prover, or outsourced-decider
  continuation quantifier;
- every retained hash, setup, extraction, and heuristic premise;
- the update-verifier and final-decider semantics; and
- separately typed completeness, soundness/knowledge, and efficiency
  conclusions.

The existing `AnalysisSemanticBasis -> AnalysisSupportInstantiation ->
AnalysisValidationBasis -> AnalysisJudgmentRecord` path forms one conditional
judgment per exact conclusion. There is no additional theorem-attachment
identity. Application does not establish theorem truth. Imported theorem truth
remains a retained premise unless an accepted proof authority discharges it.

Live `CheckedCausalPlanStepRecurrence` records are intentionally absent from
every theorem and judgment identity. They are nonidentified, nonserializable
occurrence facts. A finite chain checker may conjoin them for one process and verify exact
adjacency, but that result cannot form or prove an all-depth theorem.

### Exact occurrence support across the Relations/Analysis boundary

**Selected owners:** Relations for result export; Analysis for source
selection and support.

Relations exposes three closed owner-local checked-result source families:
causal one-step recurrence, recursion-binding coverage, and CycleFold
same-step grounding. They reuse the Foundation owner-local envelope but have
separate exact question coordinates, complete-result manifests, owner payloads,
requirements, closures, and purpose-bound fresh capabilities. There is no
generic checked-result family and no partial result projection.

Analysis uses one exact two-slot source profile for ordinary recurrence plus
coverage and a separate one-slot CycleFold profile. A concrete semantic-read
manifest fixes the static owner coordinates; support must receive the identical
owner-local result bindings and fresh capabilities. The resulting support and
occurrence judgment are local and cannot be hashed or cold-replayed. A theorem
schema, family, question, proposition, or semantic basis remains portable
because locality propagates only forward from support.

### Portable re-admission versus causal handoff

**Selected owners:** Realization for bytes and delivery; PIR for the new input
occurrence; Relations/Analysis for decider or proof validation.

Serialization produces inert material. On receipt, the material is decoded,
validated by the named decider or proof verifier, and admitted as a new ordinary
input occurrence. This is the deployment path for mutually distrustful or
different-process provers.

The path does not recreate the consumed `CausalPlanGenerationCapability`, the
source occurrence, or `CheckedPlanWitnessHandoffCorrespondence`. Byte equality,
a source receipt, and even a successful decider are insufficient to claim the
same-process causal proposition. A future transport theorem may relate the two
value-level propositions; it still cannot mint the old capability.

The proposed serializable causal-authority carrier is rejected for v0. It
would make process-local occurrence identity reproducible from portable data
and would silently change the meaning of the existing handoff judgment.

### Outstanding obligations and report qualification

**Selected owner:** Analysis, with obligation coordinates derived from exact
PIR claim meanings and Relations propositions.

An accepted accumulation update may leave decider, accumulator-validity,
hash-binding, theorem-truth, or model-instantiation obligations outstanding.
The set is finite, canonical, and exact. Each entry contains the proposition
ID, the carried public coordinate, and the operation that may discharge it.

The report states are `Conditional`, `CarriedObligationsDischarged`, and
`HypothesisFree`. Clearing the exact carried entries does not erase theorem
truth, hash, model, setup, or extraction hypotheses. `HypothesisFree` is
available only when no hypothesis remains. Missing, duplicated, mismatched, or
extra discharges refuse; callers cannot author a smaller set. PIR terminal
acceptance and Relations recurrence are not rewritten by this reporting law.

### CycleFold same-step guardrail

**Selected owner:** Relations as a protocol-specific elaboration prerequisite,
not a generic second run.

A complete CycleFold witness must show that the companion curve-operation
instance:

1. is created in the same primary step;
2. is strict rather than an unconstrained relaxed input;
3. exposes public I/O exactly equal to `(rho, C1, C2, C')` used by the primary
   fold; and
4. is folded into the companion running instance before that same step
   completes.

Passing a companion value across an accepted-terminal handoff is insufficient.
A two-run elaboration is admissible only with an exact Relations equation and
ordering result proving all four conditions; otherwise it recreates the
unbound-instance shape.

## Multi-input composition

Arity is not a missing primitive. The existing Plan preparation operation
already accepts an exact finite map of one-use handoff supplies from distinct
source continuations. The executable package therefore locks in a two-source
to one-target positive control. Tree PCD still requires a theorem over the
resulting family and recurrence discipline; accepting two inputs proves no
such theorem.

## Identity and profile consequences

The six stable upstream PIR profiles remain unchanged because none of these
decisions changes `InteractiveCore`, either Fiat--Shamir construction, public
setup, commitment opening, or Oracle commitment meaning.

Durable promotion will rotate only the downstream owners whose exact law
sources change:

- the Relations profile for binding coverage, the causal-recurrence and
  CycleFold question coordinates, and the three exact result-source families;
- the new Analysis incremental-composition and source-validation profiles for
  family uniformity, theorem application, and outstanding obligations; and
- dependent Compiler/Realization profiles that directly consume those laws.

No published identity may include a live run, capability, recurrence record,
or checked obligation discharge. Only closed families, schemas, the existing
Analysis basis/validation/judgment subjects, and their declared dependencies are
semantic identity candidates.

## Deliberate nonclaims

This model does not establish:

- an IVC, NIVC, PCD, folding, accumulation, or recursive-proof theorem;
- collision resistance or random-oracle realization;
- satisfiability or preservation of any step relation;
- continuation by arbitrary third parties;
- polynomial-depth extraction from a constant-depth theorem;
- a generic CycleFold implementation; or
- equivalence between transported input validity and same-process causality.

The focused evaluator checks that these distinctions are representable and
that their named mutations are refused. It is not a proof of the durable laws.
