### Evidence vocabulary

R2 uses five non-interchangeable evidence descriptions:

1. a **frozen replay-verified corpus** is a published case set reproduced from
   frozen public inputs by the replay contract named by that witness;
2. a **published narrow case oracle** freezes only the cases its report emits
   and says nothing about unreported modules or judgments;
3. **unit-asserted behavior** is behavior reached by a local test suite but not
   necessarily published or independently replayed; and
4. a **closed diagnostic classification** accounts for every declared code by
   authored class, but does not show that constructible codes have executed
   drivers or reach their intended first boundary; and
5. a **live diagnostic snapshot** measures the current checkout and may reveal
   coverage or reachability gaps, but is not itself a closure decision.

`Declared but unreached` is a source-surface observation, not proof that a
judgment is impossible or defective. Conversely, a fired result is not a
claim that its boundary is the right one for an invariant.

## 2. Method

The work order is:

1. reconstruct source facts and classify each as recovered, theorem premise,
   convention, local choice, or open;
2. close the minimum typed semantics demanded by the witness;
3. run well-formed negative mutations at named semantic boundaries;
4. compare Fresh/FS subjects and relation strengths without conflating them;
5. pressure identity and Relations bridges; and
6. freeze decisive results, failed gates, and the next materially different
   witness.

The probe must not import the current MLIR dialect, `SealBattery`, canonical
encoder, Python oracle, generated vocabulary objects, or target-document data
classes. It may parse a frozen fixture and construct a separately declared
finite scenario whose correspondence to that fixture is checked. Matching
current output is neither necessary nor sufficient.

## 3. Required pressure

The typed kernel must represent Statement occurrences, private/public inputs,
actor visibility, strategies, transcript influence and framing, Fresh and FS
realizations, deterministic verifier actions, public-coin eligibility, closed
codecs/samplers/algorithms/regimes, typed checks and reductions, and
source/anchor provenance. Admission, execution, relation correspondence,
theorem applicability, and quantitative Analysis remain different judgments.
Finite probes check only their named closed schedules, capability transitions,
or response plans against Core order. Durable semantics must expose execution
as a relation parameterized by protocol, strategy, inputs, and verifier coins;
Analysis must quantify over an admitted causal or adversarial strategy class.

Required mutations cover: omitted, delayed, duplicated, wrongly encoded, or
substituted Statement influence; Wire-only `g1` or nonce; challenge-namespace
collision; verifier-private influence; future reads by `g1`; altered routing;
unsupported or missing algorithms; resource exhaustion; lossy projection
misclassified as an isomorphism/embedding or priced without source and
occurrence premises; undeclared source reads; and prover-controlled absorption
after grinding but before protected query randomness. Each case must be
well-formed until its intended first rejection boundary.

## 4. Candidate comparison and reopened requirements

R2 compares two orthogonal axes. The subject axis asks about one shared
subject, mapped distinct subjects, or a shared interaction skeleton with
distinct Fresh/FS realizations. The relation axis separately asks for typed
structural maps, challenge-parametric interaction commutation, observation
projection, strategy equivalence, and any probabilistic or cryptographic
transport.

Grinding makes an unqualified Fresh/FS equality claim inadmissible in this
witness. The fixed FS strategy may preview transcript-derived pow values while
searching; the fixed Fresh strategy sends its nonce before later coin reveal and
declares no future-coin reads. The external Fresh tape is only one non-FS-derived
support point, so this comparison establishes neither distributional separation
nor a general non-anticipation theorem.

The current redesign must satisfy all of the following before any candidate is
counted:

- `ExecutionRequestId`, not `InvocationPackageId`, closes over the scenario,
  inputs, semantic application context, evaluator basis, resources,
  realization-local controls, `CoreDerivationKind`, source fixture, and source
  package;
- Fresh owns Core-derived coin slots and declared sequential-reveal,
  conditional-uniformity, and no-future-access laws. A concrete tape
  instantiates one support point; tape qualification does not establish its
  distribution law;
- a hybrid has separately identified skeleton and realization objects plus
  checked projection/composition laws; a hash inferred from two already-built
  records is not a factorization;
- a one-point value check is called pointwise correspondence, never a
  whole-domain embedding or isomorphism; and
- reports use a compact identity graph with bounded root identities rather
  than stable "sections" or a second serialization of traces and schemas.
  The report identity closes over those roots, and canonical replay recovers
  the full typed subjects and judgments.

This witness supports exactly one explicit `SemanticRegimeId`. Its local closed
finite-term ABI contains null, booleans, integers, UTF-8 text, bytes, canonical
sequences, and sorted string-keyed maps, with tagged length-delimited encoding
and explicit byte, node, and depth bounds. Every semantic identity binds that
regime, an ASCII domain, and the encoded preimage; unsupported regimes are
rejected. This is not a proposed universal v0 ABI.

These conditions and context-isolated replay now pass for the repaired witness.
Shared, distinct, and hybrid results are retained only at their exact finite
strength; a future failed hybrid remains open rather than being promoted by
nomenclature.
