## Purpose

Stage 3 determines how the selected Protocol factorization becomes a complete
semantic contract, how that contract is encoded by one small closed canonical
PIR, and how Protocol meaning meets relation, instance, witness,
committed-object, Fiat--Shamir, and composition semantics.

This is one co-design package. Protocol/PIR and Relations cannot converge
independently: each side constrains the identities, observations, authority,
and correspondence exposed to the other.

The package starts from the selected Stage 1
[Protocol IR Architecture](../../project/protocol-ir-architecture.md) and
Stage 2
[Transition and Bridge Architecture](../../project/transition-and-bridge-architecture.md).
Those pages remain non-normative target architecture; the current owners under
`docs/` remain authoritative until explicit cutover.

## Working rules

1. Write research and design artifacts in English.
2. Treat current specifications as semantic authority and code as
   correspondence and feasibility evidence.
3. Do not adjust the ideal target merely to reduce migration cost.
4. Reconstruct before redesign, but do not let the reconstruction bound the
   opportunity search.
5. Study primary sources and mature IR or protocol systems for both strengths
   and path-dependent pain points; do not copy a precedent by default.
6. Instantiate alternatives at enough resolution to expose identities,
   authority, observations, outcomes, closure, composition, and consumers.
7. Keep `ProtocolInterface` and `ProverPlan` as separate identity-bearing
   subjects; defer exact Plan field placement to Stage 4B while fixing only
   the semantic constraints Stage 3 owns.
8. Keep structural relations distinct from property transport, proof, and
   policy conclusions.
9. Record negative, unsupported, cannot-answer, refusal, malformed, and
   checker-failure outcomes separately.
10. Promote only reviewed conclusions. Durable pages may route to the
    temporary workspace index but must not depend on an individual file here.
