## 3. Selected architecture and validation result

Stage 4A selects Candidate B, **Federated Typed Analysis and Validated Decision
Compiler**, with bounded mechanisms absorbed from Candidates A, C, D, and E.
The semantic order is:

```text
family-owned semantic question
  + exact subject, view, model, proposition, hypotheses, and bases
  -> qualified family-specific Analysis judgment

exact transform problem + decision policy
  -> open unauthoritative production
  -> frozen proposal scope
  -> per-alternative independent PIR admission + transition qualification
  -> total alternative resolution
  -> semantic CandidateDomain D
  -> total qualification resolution
  -> comparison-alternative domain Q
  -> total assessment accounting
  -> checked decision sufficiency -> bounded qualified Compiler decision

checked DecisionPolicy + any exact reached qualified subset
  + exact reached closure results actually read + audit-relative accounting
  -> independently checked honest open report

preparation or checking failure at any reached operation -> outer outcome
```

The selection is not a popularity vote or a claim that one external system
already implements the model. Its decisive properties are:

1. Analysis families share only lifecycle structure; they retain their own
   proposition meaning, exact negatives, models, assumptions, validation, and
   trust rather than entering a universal claim language.
2. Proposition meaning, proof support, validation method, and operational
   search are separate identities. Multiple proof bases can establish one
   proposition without changing that proposition.
3. Compiler proposal generation is replaceable and unauthoritative. Semantic
   candidates exist only after independent target admission and transition
   qualification, and no failed or unresolved alternative may disappear from
   total scope accounting.
4. Closed best, Pareto, and no-eligible decisions range only over exact closed
   `D` and `Q` domains with total assessment accounting and either a
   policy-approved complete-assessment basis or an exact independently checked
   optimality, complete-Pareto, or infeasibility certificate. Incomplete search without such a basis
   produces an explicit open report, never a disguised global-optimality claim.
5. Relations retains occurrence-local confidential relation satisfaction;
   Analysis owns property judgments and property-specific transport;
   Compiler consumes only qualified inputs; no serialized artifact becomes a
   portable authority capability.
6. Analysis, Relations, and Compiler use total capability-neutral partial
   ingress before any complete checker signature is invoked. Missing,
   malformed, prohibited, or unavailable slots remain exact nonauthoritative
   outcomes; portable attempt records require every reached disclosure policy,
   consumer, and purpose, with unavailable governance defaulting owner-local.
7. Compiler values use disjoint portable and same-owner/generation local
   coordinates at every identity-bearing edge. Local taint propagates through
   exactly the later values that name it, and no local coordinate enters a
   public digest, replay bundle, persistent cache, or public decision claim.
8. Portable Compiler decision replay and selected-target cold handoff are
   separate products and permissions; the latter carries reconstruction
   material but grants neither decision nor PIR admission authority.

Candidate B passed all thirty-nine written scenarios. The control candidates
remain valuable: A contributes closed native rules and derivation DAGs; C
contributes dependency and replay graphs without universal claim authority; D
contributes checked external theorem/model/proof adapters; and E contributes
optional proof-carrying transforms, relational synthesis, symbolic compression
over an already materialized and qualified candidate image, and anytime
reports. Their rejected center-of-architecture choices and exact reversal
conditions remain recorded in convergence.
