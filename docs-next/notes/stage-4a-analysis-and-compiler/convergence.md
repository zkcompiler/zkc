## 1. Integrated decision

Select **Candidate B: federated typed Analysis followed by a validated-
decision Protocol Compiler**.

Analysis has one small common lifecycle for identities, dependencies,
qualified outcomes, capabilities, persistence, replay, and trust accounting.
It has no universal proposition payload or proof sovereignty. Every semantic
family owns its exact subjects, views, model, experiment, observer, direction,
proposition and refutation meaning, quantitative algebra, admissible bases,
and rules.

Compiler consumes those independently defined results through five authority-
separated planes:

```text
problem
  exact TransformProblem + exact DecisionPolicy

production
  replaceable and unauthoritative search, synthesis, proof, or manual proposal

proposal resolution
  frozen cycle-free ProposalScope + total AlternativeResolutionLedger

qualification and assessment
  PIR admission + exact transition checks + CompilerLegality
  + D -> total qualification resolution -> Q
  + immutable assessment inputs, constraints, objectives, and assessments

decision
  closed-decision path:
    checked D and Q + complete ledgers + exact comparison policy
    -> bounded Best | complete Pareto | NoEligibleCandidateIn<D,Q>
  independent open-report side exit:
    checked DecisionPolicy + any exact reached qualified subset
    + exact reached closure results actually read + audit-relative accounting
    -> explicitly open report

global outer outcome, not an additional plane
  preparation/check failure at any reached operation -> outer outcome
```

These planes partition semantic ownership rather than temporal stages. The
scope freezes first; every declared alternative then reaches its PIR admission
and transition-qualification terminal before the total alternative-resolution
ledger is finalized.

Adopt four bounded mechanisms inside Candidate B without adopting their
alternative semantic centers:

- from Candidate A, closed native rule bodies, explicit acyclic derivations,
  exact typed arithmetic, and checking that never depends on proof search;
- from Candidate C, typed dependency/support graphs and replay visibility,
  without making a universal Claim IR the owner of family semantics;
- from Candidate D, checked external-statement, model-correspondence, proof,
  and checker adapters, without delegating zkc proposition meaning to an
  external environment; and
- from Candidate E, optional proof-carrying proposals, relational synthesis,
  certificate-checked symbolic compression, and anytime reports, without
  requiring a certificate calculus for every family.

The selected topology is:

```text
exact admitted Stage 3 subjects
  + owner-created purpose-specific views
  + FamilySemanticProfile
  -> AnalysisQuestion -> AnalysisGoal -> AnalysisProposition
  + SemanticBasis + SupportInstantiation + ValidationBasis
  -> family-qualified Analysis result and process-local capability

admitted predecessor + TransformProblem + DecisionPolicy
  -> open producers and proposal occurrences
  -> frozen declared alternative scope
  -> per-alternative PIR admission + exact transition qualification
  -> total resolution of every alternative
  -> semantic CandidateDomain D
  -> total policy-governed qualification resolution
  -> exact ComparisonAlternativeDomain Q
  -> complete candidate-associated input and assessment ledgers
  -> checked decision-sufficiency closure -> qualified bounded decision

checked DecisionPolicy + any exact reached qualified subset
  + exact reached closure results actually read + audit-relative accounting
  -> independently checked explicit open report

preparation/check failure at any reached operation -> outer outcome
```

`RelationSatisfies` remains Relations-owned base predicate semantics over one
exact definition, instance, witness assignment, model, and occurrence.
Analysis consumes that exact result; it does not redefine satisfaction.

Stage 4B remains unactivated. Its OIR, projection, realization, supplier,
deployment, invocation, and endpoint facts may later enter an Analysis premise
or Compiler assessment only as independently qualified, explicitly associated
owner results. They never become hidden feasibility criteria and never depend
on a future Compiler assessment identity.

A portable decision identifies but does not carry its selected target. Cold
handoff therefore requires the separate portable-only
`CompilerSelectedTargetHandoffBundle<D,Q>` and independent PIR readmission;
decision-replay permission does not imply handoff. Conversely, any future
relation-facing OIR transition must receive the complete source-authority
binding and policy closure, separately fresh matching capability, and exact OIR
consumer/purpose authorization. Both seams remain dormant until Stage 4B is
separately activated.

## 4. Selected Analysis decisions

### 4.1 Family semantics with one thin lifecycle

Every family closes four independently versioned surfaces:

```text
FamilySemanticProfile
  subject, view, model, experiment, observer, proposition, refutation,
  quantities, and semantic read closure

FamilyBasisRegistry
  admitted rules, theorems, correspondences, transports, compositions,
  coverage ports, and certificate semantic languages

FamilyValidationProfile
  checker, decoder, translation, proof-rule, and validation-root contracts

FamilyOperationPolicy
  capability, disclosure, unknown-question, persistence, and replay rules
```

Only semantic meaning enters question identity. Adding a producer, proof,
checker implementation, theorem instance, adapter, cache, or replay consumer
does not silently create a new proposition.

### 4.2 Four distinct semantic and operational identities

`AnalysisQuestionId` identifies one stable experiment. `AnalysisGoalId`
identifies one hypothesis-free conclusion. `AnalysisPropositionId` identifies
that conclusion under one canonical typed hypothesis context.
`AnalysisRequestId` identifies one operational attempt or derive-within
request. The request cannot redefine the proposition, and failed search cannot
establish its negative.

### 4.3 Basis, support, validation, and dependency closure

`SemanticBasisId` identifies inference meaning. In the portable lane,
`SupportInstantiationId` identifies exact portable
`ExactCheckedResultAuthorityBinding` values, their inert
`OwnerCapabilityRequirement` values, total source-policy closure, and total
premise realization. If its preimage names an owner-local result-record
reference, Analysis instead uses
`LocalSupportInstantiationHandle`; only later preimages that name that handle
become local, and the affected chain is nonpersistable and not exactly
replayable. Fresh matching capabilities are occurrence-local operation inputs
and never enter either identity form. `ValidationBasisId` identifies the
semantic checker contract, exact `CheckerAbiId`, stable checker implementation,
checked implementation-to-contract
correspondence identities, and validation trust closure; fresh checker
execution authority remains occurrence-local. A derivation, result record,
checking occurrence, live capability, and replay bundle remain additional
non-aliasing categories.

Every truth-apt dependency is totally classified as:

- an exact established premise;
- an exact residual hypothesis inherited by the conclusion; or
- an exact definition/logic adequacy trust root.

There is no generic import, external theorem, trusted callback, or axiom bucket
that silently terminates the dependency graph.

### 4.4 Family-specific outcomes and negatives

The shared operation envelope distinguishes `Completed`, `Unsupported`,
`CannotAnswer`, `Refused`, `Malformed`, and `CheckerFailure`. Only completed
family outcomes may mint live semantic authority.

A semantic negative exists only when the family supplies a complete decision
or checked refutation. It retains the answered proposition, exact counter-
proposition, refutation relation, scope, facts, basis, and trust. An invalid
proof is ordinarily negative only for `ProofValid`; it is not the negation of
the proposition it attempted to establish.

### 4.5 Exact family boundaries

Core/Protocol equality, observer-indexed trace equality, directed refinement,
intentional-change conformance, distributional relations, and cost relations
are separate. Soundness, knowledge, completeness, zero knowledge, and later
cryptographic notions are separate experiment families rather than tags on
one scalar security result.

Fiat--Shamir and composition are factored:

```text
checked structural construction or composition
  + theorem/model applicability for one exact property family
  + exact source property premises and assumptions
  -> property-specific transport or composition result
```

Direct target analysis remains a distinct basis. A structural construction,
map, or composition never transports every property automatically.

## 5. Selected Compiler decisions

### 5.1 Problem and policy are not search

`TransformProblem` owns requested transition meaning. `DecisionPolicy` owns
domain formation, accepted qualifications/trust, required owner facts,
constraints, objectives, comparison, tie/representative rules, and requested
decision/report strength. Operational run, job, producer, recipe, seed,
budget, timeout, worker count, solver, heuristic, and discovery order belong
to replaceable production.

### 5.2 Frozen alternatives before semantic outcomes

One `ProposalScope` freezes a canonical finite descriptor sequence or exact
finite grammar before admission, relation, constraint, or score outcomes are
known. `DeclaredAlternativeId` is derived from the scope and coordinate, so no
identity cycle exists.

The total `AlternativeResolutionLedger` accounts for every alternative as
resolved, duplicate, conclusively excluded, unsupported, cannot-answer,
refused, malformed, checker-failed, or incomplete. Unsupported and unresolved
alternatives remain visible; they are not members of the semantic candidate
domain and cannot disappear from a closed originating-scope claim.

### 5.3 Independent admission, transition, and legality

PIR authenticates and admits every target and every semantically asserted
intermediate. The exact semantic owner checks each predecessor/successor
relation and path edge. Compiler only orchestrates those checks.
`CompilerLegality` separately checks TransformProblem-local restrictions.
None implies the others, property transport, assessment, endpoint feasibility,
or selection.

### 5.4 Two domains with total qualification resolution

`CandidateId` identifies `TransformProblemId + TransitionCaseId`; it excludes
producer, proposal coordinate, recipe, search history, proof basis,
assessment, score, and checking occurrence.

`CandidateDomain D` is a finite canonical image of independently admitted,
transition-qualified semantic candidates. For every candidate, a separately
identified qualification-only input projection freezes exactly the
qualification records, complete origins, policy closures, and capability
requirements read by resolution. A total `QualificationResolutionLedger<D>`
then applies the exact policy to every checked projection and derives a
separately checked `ComparisonAlternativeDomain Q`.
Normally Q selects one accepted qualification or corroborating set per
candidate; a policy may instead compare all accepted qualified alternatives.
Every Q member projects to exactly one semantic candidate.

This split prevents proof-basis choice from multiplying semantic candidates
while still allowing trust or corroboration to affect comparison explicitly.

### 5.5 Non-cyclic assessment inputs

Concrete Analysis, Relations, Evidence, and later Stage 4B values arrive in an
immutable candidate-indexed `AssessmentInputPortfolio`. Its portable content
identity excludes any completeness result; a portfolio that names a local input
uses a nonpersistable Compiler-local handle. A separate checker establishes
schema coverage, uniqueness, association, polarity, assurance, complete owner
result-origin binding, authenticated immediate and transitive source-policy
acceptance, matching capability requirements, and trust acceptance. Separate
`AssessmentInputUse` edges bind a fact and its matching live owner capability,
origin binding, and total policy closure to the policy and candidate. Peer-owner
facts never name a future `AssessmentId`.

Constraints consume exact polarity, assurance, complete owner origin, and
immediate plus transitive owner policy without widening any of them. Objectives
preserve units, provenance, model, uncertainty, epistemic kind, origin,
capability ABI, and total policy closure. Missing is not infinity; unsupported
is not false; a measurement is not a semantic theorem.

### 5.6 Closed decisions and open reports

A qualified decision binds exact D and Q, their closure bases, total
qualification and assessment-accounting ledgers, a checked sufficiency basis,
exact comparison and representative policy, selected support, and residual-
trust closure. The sufficiency basis is either policy-approved complete
assessment or an exact independently checked certificate over D and Q. It
establishes only:

- best in the exact enumerated closed domains;
- the complete Pareto frontier in those domains; or
- no eligible candidate in those domains under complete exact exclusion or
  infeasibility evidence.

A feasible candidate, subset frontier/best, or incomplete progress result is
an open non-decision report. It may be useful to an explicit consumer, but it
mints no closure, optimality, complete-frontier, or no-eligible authority.

V0 symbolic certificates may compress checks only over already materialized,
PIR-admitted, transition-qualified candidate identities. A stronger lazy
symbolic domain whose denotation includes unnamed or unadmitted Protocols
requires an explicit future reopening of the Stage 3 subject/admission model.

## 6. Ownership and authority boundaries

| Concern | Exact owner | Stage 4A use and forbidden widening |
|---|---|---|
| Protocol authentication and admission | PIR | Analysis/Compiler consume live exact capabilities; neither mints or serializes them |
| Structural and behavioral proposition meaning | Exact Analysis or bridge family | Compiler requests and consumes; policy cannot redefine it |
| Predicate satisfaction for one relation instance/witness occurrence | Relations | Analysis may consume the exact confidential qualified result; no global witness identity or unsatisfiability inference |
| Compiler-local transform restrictions | Compiler `TransformProblem` | Checked as legality; not a Protocol relation |
| Accepted basis/trust, constraints, objectives, and comparison | Compiler `DecisionPolicy` | Evaluates exact owner inputs; creates no upstream semantic authority |
| OIR, projection, realization, endpoint, and runtime facts | Future Stage 4B owners | Only exact typed later-owned inputs after separate activation and checking |
| Raw observations and measurements | The producing semantic or operational domain | Owns event/result meaning, ordering, taxonomy, sensitivity, and completeness frontier; Evidence cannot reinterpret them |
| Attributable records and policy-qualified appraisals | Evidence | Retains exact producer meaning, environment, method, sample, and uncertainty; no theorem conversion without a named Analysis rule |
| Use-specific reliance | Named later consumer | Never implied by an Analysis result or Compiler decision |

Only a successful current owner check mints a process-local capability. IDs,
records, bytes, proof files, certificates, signatures, caches, and replay
bundles are inert. Cold consumers reconstruct exact inputs and rerun the
relevant check; they do not deserialize authority.

## 7. Alternative disposition

| Candidate | Disposition | Retained mechanism | Principal reason not selected as center |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: extended closed kernel and enumerated Compiler | Not selected | Closed native rules, exact arithmetic, derivation DAGs, no-search checking | One growing universal sum/catalog couples heterogeneous families and makes extension/replay increasingly global |

Candidate B passes all thirty-nine written scenarios. Counts are diagnostic,
not a score: A has 28 pass, 9 conditional, 1 supported-with-deferral, and 1

an unrelated pass, and no count proves semantic correctness.

## 8. Capabilities enabled by the selection

The architecture makes several capabilities possible without widening their
claims:

1. one semantic proposition may be established by native, direct, external,
   certificate, or carefully defined Evidence-derived lanes while retaining
   exact assurance and trust differences;
2. a new Analysis family can be added locally without changing unrelated
   family identities or a universal optional-field record;
3. MLIR passes, e-graphs, solvers, superoptimizers, learned producers, formal
   transformers, and manual proposals can compete under the same admission,
   transition, assessment, and decision checks;
4. proof-basis trust can affect policy comparison without changing semantic
   candidate identity;
5. exact negative results can satisfy explicitly negative constraints without
   being widened to affirmative facts or global impossibility;
6. Fiat--Shamir and composition can transport each property through its own
   theorem, assumptions, and quantitative loss rather than one generic
   preservation flag;
7. useful feasible candidates and partial frontiers can be reported during
   incomplete search without claiming optimality or no-eligible closure;
8. certificate systems can compress finite-domain checks without acquiring
   authority to invent or admit Protocols; and
9. in the fully portable lane, cold replay can recheck the semantic decision
   without rerunning mutable or nondeterministic proposal discovery; a local
   confidential lane instead requires a fresh authorized rerun and new handles.

## 9. Costs and deliberate deferrals

The selected model deliberately pays for:

- more exact identities, profiles, ledgers, association edges, and closure
  checks;
- family-by-family experiment, negative, and quantitative semantics;
- heterogeneous replay bundles and explicit residual-trust union;
- two Compiler domains when qualification affects comparison; and
- bounded claims rather than informal global optimality.

Stage 4A defers concrete theorem statements, rule catalogs, proof languages,
checker implementations, certificate formats, solver choices, transform
families, optimizer algorithms, canonical wire encodings, implementation
organization, migration, compatibility, and cost estimates.

It also defers all Stage 4B OIR/Realization semantics, concrete Evidence
schemas, policies, method profiles, and checkers, reliance policy, normative
consolidation, and implementation correspondence.
Deferral never weakens a selected semantic distinction into ambient metadata.

## 10. Current-to-target correspondence

The [current-to-target gap](current-to-target-gap.md) classifies current roles
as retained, split, replaced, new, or deferred without defining a migration.
The target retains the current system's strongest disciplines: exact admitted
subjects, immutable rule catalogs, closed rule bodies, explicit derivations,
visible hypotheses, exact arithmetic, finite comparison scopes, deterministic
tie handling, untrusted producers, independently authenticated targets, and
fresh decision recomputation.

The target primarily splits roles that the current model couples:

- one Soundness-specific context becomes family semantic, basis, validation,
  support, operation, and trust contracts;
- Compiler-owned property derivation becomes consumption of independently
  identified Analysis results;
- provider enumeration becomes open production plus frozen scope, total
  resolution, semantic D, policy-derived Q, total assessment accounting, and a
  checked assessment-sufficiency basis;
- candidate identity separates from proof basis, assessment, score, and
  operational search; and
- serialized results become replay inputs rather than portable authority.

This classification is a semantic delta, not an implementation plan. It does
not choose compatibility shims, migration order, code modules, wire formats,
or feasibility priorities.

## 11. Reversal and reopening conditions

The selected center must be reopened if equal-resolution evidence shows any of
the following:

1. a genuinely smaller universal logic represents every selected structural,
   probabilistic, cryptographic, cost, Fiat--Shamir, composition, negative,
   occurrence, and trust distinction without hidden optional semantics and
   with a smaller audited trust base;
2. one external environment covers every required zkc proposition with fully
   checked statement/model correspondence, stable exact identity, and
   acceptable cold replay;
3. certificate systems cover the complete Analysis and Compiler surface more
   simply than mixed native/direct/external/certificate lanes;
4. federation causes material semantic duplication that exact cross-family
   reuse cannot safely express through named implication, transport, or
   composition rules;
5. the common lifecycle cannot remain small without recreating a universal
   payload or generic authority capability;
6. an exact Compiler case requires proof basis to be part of semantic
   candidate identity rather than a qualification or assessment coordinate;
7. a Stage 4B product-domain design makes OIR, realization, or endpoint
   configuration part of semantic candidate identity rather than an explicit
   assessment input; or
8. an exact contradiction with a frozen Stage 1--3 subject, identity, view,
   admission, relation, occurrence, authority, or replay decision appears.

A new producer, checker, proof assistant, solver, optimization heuristic,
implementation constraint, or migration inconvenience is not a reopening
reason by itself. It may narrow a supported lane while preserving the selected
semantic architecture.
