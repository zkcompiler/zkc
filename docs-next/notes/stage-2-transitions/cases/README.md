> **Document kind:** Temporary research index

## Purpose

This directory studies primary research and official specifications that can
inform exact Stage 2 questions about transition relations, checking placement,
capability continuity, replay, evolution, provenance, and evidence. Every case
must state both the useful mechanism and the limit of the analogy.

## Inventory

- [Transition and Checking Models](transition-and-checking-models.md) —
  translation validation, refinement, certificates, MLIR legality,
  capabilities, serialization, versioning, and provenance.

## Use rule

External mechanisms may expose a candidate, theorem shape, validation
boundary, or long-term cost. They cannot prove that the current implementation
has that property, replace either endpoint's semantic owner, or settle a zkc
design by reputation.

The dossier was reviewed during Stage 2 convergence. Its transferable lessons
were absorbed into the durable
[Transition and Bridge Architecture](../../../project/transition-and-bridge-architecture.md).
This directory remains temporary research evidence and will be deleted with
the Stage 2 package after later owners have absorbed their exact seams.
